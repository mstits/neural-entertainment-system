## Synthesis

**5 of 10 games landed SOUND_ADVANCING** (1942, Blaster Master, Bionic Commando, Batman — preliminary, Paperboy). 2 are CAMERA_STATIC_AGENT_ACTIVE (Ice Climber, Galaga — both genuinely fixed-camera genres, not walls). 2 are SOUND_GAME_STOPS skill-walls with a diagnosed, receipted cause (Arkanoid: one-life ball-tracking difficulty; Chip 'n Dale: camera-clamp + a hazard just past the cap). 1 is BLOCKED on missing tooling rather than any game property (Tetris).

**Mechanisms that earned their keep this wave:**
- The **signed-odometer-axis fix** (`axis: -y` in `go_explore_solve.py`, backward-compatible via `getattr`) turned 1942 from a 7-cell freeze into 106 cells in the same wall-clock budget — a real code defect fixed generically, not a game-specific hack, verified against the full test suite (609 passed).
- **`discover_observables.py`'s 3-probe protocol** was the workhorse: it was the only thing standing between a crash (`KeyError: 'y'`) and a runnable smoke for at least six profiles this wave (Ice Climber, Arkanoid, Blaster Master, Bionic Commando, Batman, Paperboy, Chip 'n Dale) that had only `progress: {source: odometer}` in their `solve:` block. This is now a confirmed, recurring gap in how odometer-only profiles get minted, not a one-off — Galaga was flagged as carrying the same latent gap even though it wasn't hit this run.
- The **deepest-cell diagnostic pattern** (new per-game scripts modeled on `diag_1942_deepest.py`) correctly separated three different flat-frontier causes that look identical from the outside: a scripted non-interactive window (Ice Climber), an automatic scene transition that isn't death (Bionic Commando's lives 1→2), and a real hazard past a camera cap (Chip 'n Dale). None of these would have been distinguishable from `progress.jsonl` alone.
- The **scene-key / room-counter (`area:`) pattern** (Ninja Gaiden/Metroid precedent) correctly caught camera-clamp saturation in Blaster Master, Chip 'n Dale, and Bionic Commando before it could be mistaken for a wall.

**One-mechanism-away:** Tetris and Galaga are both blocked on the same missing capability — a purity-line-compliant HUD/tile digit-decode discovery tool for decimal score/line/wave counters, the class of fix DuckTales already needed a bespoke tile adapter for. Chip 'n Dale's scene-key fix is applied but empirically unconfirmed (the counter never incremented in any receipt) and just needs a longer run to validate or refute. Batman's SOUND_ADVANCING call is provisional pending the smoke finishing past its 120s snapshot. Ice Climber needs a jump-capable driver, not a new mechanism, to re-probe for its true (non-scroll) progress axis.
