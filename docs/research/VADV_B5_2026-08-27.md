## 4. What can be said without moving a goalpost

1. **The critic is not flat at the wall.** `WALL` reads LIVE against its own
   permutation null, and A7 (power 1.0) shows that is not a thin-data artifact.
   **The strong form of the mis-specification story — "the critic cannot tell the
   actions apart at gx 2674" — is not what the data shows.**
2. **But the signal there is markedly weaker, in the direction mis-specification
   predicts.** The action-discrimination excess at the wall is **27.9 % of** the
   excess in the band B5's own curriculum demonstrably advanced through. `WALL`'s
   LIVE is also *marginal* — 0.1329 against a threshold of 0.1230, an 8 % margin,
   and only 1.29× its own analytic H0 expectation — where `PC_B5`'s is decisive
   (0.307 vs 0.110, 2.8×). This is a real difference at a magnitude the
   registration declared insufficient to act on. It is recorded, not acted on.
3. **The omission of `r(s,a)` biases against the more interesting conclusion.**
   Registered in advance: a progress-based reward contributes more action-variance
   where progress is achievable than at a progress-flat wall, so omitting it makes
   `R` *larger*. The true `R` is likely below 0.279. The measured indeterminacy is
   therefore not an artifact of a thumb on the scale toward re-opening B5.
4. **Low power biases toward COLLAPSED**, the more interesting conclusion — which
   is exactly why A7 was binding, and why its passing at power 1.0 makes this
   ambiguity **genuine rather than a sample-size artifact**.

## 5. Four limits found in the receipts, disclosed rather than exploited

### 5.1 The critic-fit confound is not controlled, and it is the binding one

**This data cannot separate a reward-mis-specification collapse from a critic
that simply never fit the wall states.**

*Controlled:* the **global** form. One critic scores both bands on one bank and
`R` is a within-checkpoint ratio, so a uniform fit failure depresses numerator and
denominator alike and cancels. A5 passed, so the critic is not dead. A3 proves a
dead critic returns VOID, not a false COLLAPSED.

*Not controlled:* the **local** form. The banks are go-explore expert-window
states, **not B5's own on-policy rollouts**, so wall states are off-distribution
for this critic. A critic that never fit those states — *because B5's policy
arrived there 0/717 times* — shows depressed action-discrimination for reasons
with nothing to do with the reward function. And "the policy never got there" is
precisely what CAPABILITY asserts. A depressed V_adv at the wall is therefore
jointly predicted by (a) no reward gradient, (b) critic under-fit from lack of
visitation, and (c) capability failure *causing* (b). **The instrument cannot rank
these three on this data.** This is a second, independent reason the result could
not have carried a verdict even had `R` fallen outside the indeterminate band —
and it is what makes an on-policy bank the highest-value follow-up rather than
merely a nice-to-have.

### 5.2 The band is 198 px wide; only its entry pixel was measured

Every one of the 612 qualifying `WALL` rows decodes to **gx = 2674 exactly**
(`runs/vadv_b5/WALL/wall_receipt.json`: `n_distinct = 1`, `min = max = 2674`).
The interior is *visited* — 49 rows at 2676, singletons scattered out to 2862 —
but never revisited under more than one action, so nothing there can qualify under
the per-cell rule. **"V_adv at the wall" is really "V_adv at the wall's entry
state." The instrument never observed the traversal B5 claims cannot be
executed.** This under-weights what the summary rows suggest and must be fixed
before any future reading is treated as a reading of the wall itself.

### 5.3 `R` is not robust to the choice of null reference

The instrument emits two nulls. Against the **permutation medians** (the
registered reference) `R = 0.279` → VOID. Against the **analytic df-based nulls**
the same data gives `R = (0.1329 − 0.1027)/(0.3073 − 0.0358) = 0.111` → which
would cross into **MIS-SPECIFICATION**.

The registered reference is the permutation median, so **0.279 governs and the
verdict is VOID.** This is disclosed rather than exploited: the statistic is not
re-read to reach the more interesting conclusion. But a future registration must
**pre-commit to the null reference explicitly**, because this one unstated choice
moves the answer across a decision boundary.

---

### 5.4 Two different `Var_batch[V]` values circulate, and they are not interchangeable

The normaliser is pooled **once per scoring call, over the union of the bands that
call scored**. The §2 call scored only `WALL` + `PC_B5` and so reports
`Var_batch[V] = 4.763`; the registration's wider pool reports `8979.1`; the 1-1
and early-1-2 checkpoints report `35810.4` and `7831.1`. Anyone reading `raw` or
`raw_norm` across those receipts will compare numbers that share no denominator —
`raw` at the wall is `9.36e-05` and at `PC_B5` is `0.104`, and neither means
anything on its own.

This does **not** touch the reading. η² is exactly invariant under `V → cV + b`,
and the §2 η² values match the registration's to three significant figures across
a ~1900× difference in pooled variance — which is the invariance working, observed
rather than asserted. The verdict statistic `R` is a within-call ratio, so the
denominator cancels identically. Recorded because a future reader who reaches for
`raw` will otherwise compare incomparable quantities, and because it is the
cleanest empirical evidence in this job that the normalisation choice made in the
registration was the load-bearing one.

## 6. The reusable point — and it is the fourth instance today

> **An instrument adopted in a pre-registration and never built is
> indistinguishable, in the receipts, from one that was built and returned
> nothing.**

Both leave the same trace: a registration that names the instrument, and no
number. Under the once-rule the second closes a question; the first only *looks*
like it did. B5 CLOSED under the once-rule with its own discriminator unwritten,
and nothing in the receipt chain distinguished that from a discriminator that ran
and found nothing — which is why the claim stood for 17 days.

This is the **fourth instance of the pattern found today**, and the four span the
full spectrum from "never written" to "written, tested, and mute":

| # | instrument | how it was inert | trace it left |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ReDo** (v27 / v28) | built and armed, but at **τ = 0.025 against a ≥ 0.25 firing threshold** — outside its own acting range, so it never fired | two FAIL verdicts that were never tests of the plasticity hypothesis |
| 2 | **v22 semi-MDP GAE** (`src/training/smdp_gae.py`) | implemented, unit-tested, **imported by nothing but its own test** | an options A/B recorded as FAIL, crediting a mechanism the experiment never ran |
| 3 | **confluence null-rate gate** (`solve.null_rates`) | armed in code, **no profile supplies the rates**, so `DEGENERATE` can never trigger | `clear_quorum` arming receipts that mean nothing; a Contra VOID found only after 2.4M worker-steps |
| 4 | **V_adv** (this document) | **adopted in `v15_d1` and never written**; parameter drift substituted in as the fifth instrument | a never-retracted "REAL CAPABILITY WALL" |

The defect is structural, not four separate lapses: **a registration records what
an instrument is supposed to do; only the receipt records what it did, and an
absent receipt reads as a silent pass.** The mitigation that generalises is the
one already applied here — every threshold stated as a **ratio against a reference
measured on the same data**, plus an emitted `eta2_null_analytic` on every reading
so the gap between observed and its own null is visible on the face of the output.
That is what made it possible to state, in §5.3, that the null reference was
load-bearing. An absolute constant would have hidden it exactly the way ReDo's τ
did.

Logged to `MISTAKES.md` under `[inert-treatment]`.
