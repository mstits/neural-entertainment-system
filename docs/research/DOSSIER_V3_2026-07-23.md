## 2. The five exploits (each verified, each fixed)

The policy *was* optimizing exactly what we wrote. Every plateau traced to a
defect in the shaping, not the learner. In chronological order:

### 2.1 Farmable negative potential (from v2, recap)
Φ(s) = −0.15·D with the terminal term skipped on death ⇒ greedy banked +168
of un-canceled approach shaping and died at gx 183. **Fix:** positive-shifted
Φ ∈ [0,100], death ⇒ F = −Φ(s). Verified by telescoping-sum test
(progress-then-die = −30 ≤ 0; progress-to-clear = +50).

### 2.2 Distance-map aliasing at area transitions
SMB reads x=0,y=0 for ~6 frames whenever an area loads (pipe transitions
reset the scroll). All loading frames collapse to cell key `(area, 0, 0)`.
In 1-2 the area-2 *entry* (~700 steps remaining) and a later internal pipe
(~262 remaining) both read `(2,0,0)`; min-over-visits poisoned the entry cell
to D=235. Result: Φ spiked to 68.6 on entering the underground, then **every
real forward step dropped Φ by ~56** — the shaping actively punished progress
at the area boundary, exactly where the v2 recurrent run's greedy died
(gx 196). **Fix:** `build_distance_map` skips x==0∧y==0 frames. Verified:
entry region now monotone (Φ 12.7→14.7 forward). *The v2 run you diagnosed
was poisoned by this too.*

```python
# src/utils/wavefront_reward.py (fix)
if _x(ram) == 0 and int(ram[R_YPOS]) == 0:
    skipped += 1  # loading/transition frame — do not record (aliases)
```

### 2.3 X-only projection ⇒ the ceiling route
Off-map states projected to the nearest same-area x-bucket *ignoring y*. The
1-2 **ceiling** (never visited by any solution; expert envelope at that
stretch is y-bands 4–5 only) therefore inherited the floor's full forward
gradient at every x — with **zero death risk** (no enemies up there). Under
sticky training the risk-averse ceiling was rational; it dead-ends into the
warp room ~200 steps later (invisible at γ=0.99). Physical diagnostic
(pmax=1.00): greedy ran the floor to x≈865, jumped onto the ceiling, dropped
into the warp room, wandered to timeout — every episode. **Fix:** y-aware
geodesic projection — inherit only from cells within ±1 y-band AND pay a
travel cost (D_start/204 per x-bucket); outside the envelope ⇒ Φ=0.
Verified: floor unchanged, climbing on costs −8 immediately, ceiling strictly
below floor at every x.

### 2.4 The Φ=0 sanctuary
Non-clear terminals charged −Φ(final). The warp room has Φ=0 ⇒ **timing out
while hiding there was free** (vs −34 for timing out on-path, −49 for dying
while pushing). The policy found it and moved in. **Fix:** non-clear
terminals charge **−Φ_peak** (episode max) — die, idle, retreat, or hide, a
non-completing episode nets ≤ 0 shaping; plus a lost-cut (Φ≤0.5 for 150
consecutive steps ⇒ terminate as lost), which also reclaimed the ~70% of
rollout steps burned wandering off-path.

### 2.5 (Harness, not reward) Random-net probes
Our custom probe passed a checkpoint *path* (not the loaded dict) to
`build_tile_policy_from_checkpoint` and never called `load_state_dict` — it
measured a random-init net for several hours (symptom: minD identical across
stochastic episodes; policy entropy pinned at ln 6). Fixed and now asserts
non-init weights. All numbers in this dossier are from the fixed harness.
The official `eval_game.py` was never affected.

objective — see §5.

`robustify_level.py` ladder mode: rungs along the solution (deepest first),

| v | Collection noise | Handover | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | sticky 0.25 + ε 0.15 everywhere | none | starved: 0 clears/400 at gx 1634; bridge also 0/400 |

Two findings of consequence:

**4.3 The perfect-clone result.** At rung gx 1842, BC reached train acc
1.000 (loss 4e-4) on demos *collected from that exact rung* — and scored
**0.00** under sticky-0.25 acceptance from the same start state. A perfect
memorization of a working trajectory has ~zero probability of surviving 25%
action-repeat noise through this segment. This is the honest-protocol wall
reproduced in a 200-px microcosm, free of all confounds.

**4.4 The gate-mirage result.** v3's per-rung acceptance (3 sticky episodes)
passed rungs whose *true* sticky success is < 0.25% (0/400 measured from gx
3154, deep inside the accepted basin). Small-sample acceptance gates are
worthless at these success rates; every prior per-level "weld" claim that
used few-episode gates should be assumed inflated (consistent with the
project's older die-respawn/eval-inflation history).

## 5. The reframe and the constrained question for you

Facts to reconcile:
- 1-1 IS solvable to 63–67% cold sticky+jitter by from-scratch reactive PPO
  (the one genuine success; no shaping, no demos).
- 1-2's gauntlet (gx 1600–2200) has per-attempt sticky success < 1/400 for
  every near-open-loop policy we can construct (clones, welds, anchored
  PPO argmax), and PPO-with-everything-fixed plateaus at its head (36%).
- Imitation cannot exceed ~84% agreement for information-theoretic reasons
  (§3), and 84%-agreement policies traverse *less* than RL policies.
- The obs space is NOT the constraint (§3.6 null); action history helps a
  little; recurrence trains too slowly to matter at our budgets.
- All shaping is now provably non-farmable; training clears flow only from
  deep restarts.

Our current best mental model: **1-2's gauntlet demands genuinely closed-loop
recovery behavior with a per-step precision that sticky-0.25 stresses far
beyond anything 1-1 requires.** The training signal for that behavior cannot
come from imitation (ill-posed) or from entrance rollouts (never reach it
under sticky); it must come from dense RL practice *at the gauntlet itself*
— which our uniform-frontier Go-Explore restarts provide only diffusely.

Questions, tightly constrained (no game internals; everything must be
search-derived or generic):

1. **Restart concentration:** what is the principled schedule for
   concentrating restart density at the *stall frontier* (e.g., sampling
   restarts ∝ TD-error or ∝ 1/success-rate per cell) rather than uniform
   frontier selection? Is there a known convergent variant (Prioritized
   Level Replay is level-granular; we need *cell*-granular within a level)?
2. **Success amplification at ~0 success:** from a gauntlet cell, sticky
   success for the current policy is ≪ 1/400. What is SOTA for bootstrapping
   in this regime — automatic curriculum on noise (anneal sticky 0→0.25 per
   cell as success rises), hindsight relabeling on distance-to-goal, or
   return-conditioned/upside-down formulations? Give the concrete pick for a
   712-d obs feedforward PPO stack at ~4k steps/s.
3. **Sticky-noise curriculum legitimacy:** if we anneal sticky per restart
   cell (train at the noise level the cell can currently survive, raising it
   to 0.25 before "passing" it), the FINAL eval remains cold sticky-0.25
   from the entrance. Does any published protocol object to noise curricula
   in training? (We believe not — Salimans & Chen anneal the start state,
   not the noise — but flag any precedent.)
4. **Acceptance statistics:** given per-segment success rates possibly ≪1%,
   what gate (episode count / sequential test) makes a weld claim
   trustworthy at reasonable compute? We propose Wilson lower-bound ≥ 50%
   at 95% on ≥ 20 episodes; sanity-check this.
5. **Is 1-2-under-sticky simply long-tailed?** Estimate: if a policy has
   per-segment sticky-survival p across k independent precision segments,
   entrance success ≈ Πpₖ. If the gauntlet's best-achievable p for ANY
   policy in this class is, say, 0.3, the honest bar (≥60%) is unreachable
   and the target itself needs re-negotiation (e.g., report sticky-0.10, or
   median-of-N attempts, alongside). Do you know of ANY published agent —
   any method, any compute — that clears SMB 1-2 specifically under
   Machado sticky-0.25 from the level start? (Our search found none: 1-2 is
   where sticky results stop being reported.)
