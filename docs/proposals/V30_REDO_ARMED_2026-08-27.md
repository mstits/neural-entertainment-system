## 1. Root cause of the inertness — architectural, not a typo

`TilePolicyNetwork` is `Linear → LayerNorm → SiLU`
(`src/models/tile_policy.py:104-105`). Sokar's dormancy is a **ReLU**
phenomenon: a hard zero with an exactly-zero gradient is an absorbing
state, and the 0.025–0.1 threshold is calibrated to that. This trunk has
no absorbing state:

* SiLU is smooth and nonzero for all finite `x` (minimum ≈ −0.278 at
  `x ≈ −1.278`); a unit pushed negative keeps passing gradient.
* Pre-activation LayerNorm re-normalizes to zero-mean / unit-variance
  **across units, every forward pass**, so no unit's activation magnitude
  can decay away relative to its layer.

Under Definition 1 with layer-mean normalization
(`src/training/redo.py:67-82`, correctly implemented — the DR's "layer's
maximum" phrasing is the error, and the repo already registered that
correction), scores bunch near 1.0 and the left tail begins empirically
around 0.25. **The DR's prescribed range is not merely unlucky on this
stack; it is unreachable.** The eight runs' 2,000 zero-recycle checks are
the proof.

This is the first finding of v30 and it is already banked, at zero
compute cost.

## 2. tau — the operating point

**tau = 0.25.** Registered. `redo_check_every_iters: 1`,
`redo_sample_batch: 4096`, `redo_reset_optimizer_moments: true` —
otherwise the v27 block verbatim.

Justification against the DR's own prescribed range: 0.025–0.1 is
provably inert here (§1), so executing the prescription **as written**
requires translating its *intent* — recycle the bottom tail of the
activation distribution — onto this activation function's score scale.
The translation is done by measurement, not by re-deriving the paper's
ReLU constant. 0.25 is the **smallest threshold on the sweep that fires
at all**: the minimum dose.

**The risk I am taking is the inert-side risk, deliberately.** The sweep
is thin — 2 iterations, near init, on a *throwaway 64/32 profile*, while
the arm runs **96/32**. tau 0.25 may not fire on the real width. I accept
that risk because §3's arming gate reduces its cost to **~10 minutes**.

**The risk I am refusing is over-recycling.** At 0.30 the second check
already takes 13 of 32 trunk units and greedy-argmax agreement falls to
0.643; at 0.50 it takes 18–23 of 32 **every iteration, repeating the same
index set** (`pilot_redo_forced.log`: fc2 = [1,2,4,5,7,9,13,16,…] at iter
0, 1, 2, 3) — that is not recycling dormant units, it is a per-iteration
partial reset of two-thirds of the trunk, which is precisely the "network
reset" family the DR ruled **INCOMPATIBLE** in its own §4. A FAIL under
that dose would be uninterpretable — "ReDo damaged a healthy network" and
"plasticity was not the barrier" would be inseparable. That is the exact
disease this workflow exists to cure, so I will not reintroduce it.

**Pre-registered escalation, exactly one step, fixed now.** If Phase M
(§6) or the iter-25 arming gate voids at 0.25, tau escalates to **0.30**,
the arm is re-run once, and the identity abort (§7 A4) becomes live. If
0.30 also voids, the campaign reports "no dormancy substrate exists on
this architecture" and stops. **tau ≥ 0.35 is forbidden by this
registration.** A finite written ladder with a stopping rule is not a
moving goalpost; anything beyond it would be.

## 3. The armed check — `cum_recycled > 0`, in code, three places

A run in which ReDo does not fire is **VOID, not FAIL**. Enforced by
machinery, not intention.

**(1) In-run deadline — the one that saves the budget.**
`src/training/trainer.py`, immediately after
`self._redo_cum_recycled += _rd.recycled` (currently line ~7852). New
module constant `_REDO_ARM_DEADLINE_ITERS = 25`, **hardcoded, not a
config key** — 20 declared config keys in the flagship recipe never
executed, and a new declared key is a new way to be inert.

```
if redo_on and global_it >= _REDO_ARM_DEADLINE_ITERS \
        and self._redo_cum_recycled == 0:
    raise RuntimeError(
        f"[redo] VOID: armed at tau={self.redo_tau} but cum_recycled==0 "
        f"after {global_it + 1} iterations. ReDo does not fire below "
        f"tau~0.25 on Linear->LayerNorm->SiLU; receipts: "
        f"runs/v27_fresh_recovery/preflight/redo_forced/isolate_tau*.log. "
        f"This run is VOID, not FAIL. Do not issue a verdict."
    )
```

Cost of a mis-armed arm: 25 iters × 25.4 s ≈ **10.6 minutes**, versus the
1 h 47 m per seed / 7 h 10 m per arm that v27 and v28 each burned inert.
The launcher aborts the whole 4-seed sequence on this exception, so no
sibling seed inherits the defect.

**(2) At verdict time.** `scripts/redo_arm_gate.py` (new). For every
seed's `run.log` it requires all of:
* one `[redo] ENABLED tau=0.25 …` line, and **no** `[redo] disabled`;
* `>= 10` `[redo] iter N: … recycled M …` lines with `M >= 1`;
* final `cum >= 20`.

Any failure prints `VERDICT: VOID (redo never fired)` or
`VERDICT: VOID-MINIMAL-DOSE` and **exits 2**. It is structurally
incapable of printing PASS or FAIL for an unarmed seed. The `>= 10
events / >= 20 units` floor closes the "technically fired once" loophole
— which is exactly how the eighth vacuous gate gets written.

**(3) In the receipt.** `run_manifest.json` gains `redo_tau`,
`redo_cum_recycled`, `redo_recycle_events`, `redo_first_recycle_iter`,
`redo_median_agree`. A later reader cannot mistake an inert run for a
tested one, which is the failure mode that cost this campaign 14 hours.

**Anti-vacuity — the revert-verified failure ships with the check.**
`tests/test_redo_armed_gate.py`:
* `test_deadline_raises_at_v27_tau` — 30-iteration smoke run with
  `redo_tau: 0.025` asserts `RuntimeError` is raised. Reverting the
  deadline makes it fail.
* `test_gate_voids_the_historical_v27_log` — feeds the **actual banked**
  `checkpoints/mario_1_1_v27_recovery_seed0/run.log` (`cum 0` on every
  line) to `redo_arm_gate.py` and asserts exit 2 with `VOID`, not FAIL.
  The new gate, applied to the historical failure, reproduces the correct
  verdict. This is the strongest anti-vacuity receipt available.

**Structural fix to the V7 class of vacuity.** V7 required `agree >=
0.98` at every recycle event *and* `>= 1` recycle event — on this
architecture those are near mutually exclusive, so it was satisfied by
piloting at tau **0.5, twenty times the registered operating point**.
New standing rule, registered here: **every preflight condition is
evaluated at exactly the registered operating point; a preflight that
passes at any other tau is VOID.** The identity criterion is therefore
re-registered at a value achievable at the operating tau (§7 A4,
`median agree >= 0.60`), and the fact that 0.98 is unachievable at any
firing tau is recorded as a finding: **ReDo's identity-preservation
guarantee does not hold under pre-activation LayerNorm.**

## 4. The bar

Statistic and numerals are **unchanged from v27 and v28** so the verdict
is comparable to the runs it is judged against. warp_rate must be 0.0.

* **PASS**: Θ ≥ **0.80**
* **FAIL**: Θ ≤ **0.767**
* **MARGINAL**: 0.767 < Θ < 0.80 — reported as MARGINAL, never as PASS,
  and it does not authorize any follow-on claim.

**Θ = best-of-4 over seeds of the cross-fit split-sample honest clear
rate.** The estimator is stricter than the one v27/v28 were scored under;
a PASS under a stricter estimator is a stronger claim, never a weaker
one, so tightening it is admissible where loosening would not be.

**Estimator, fixed in advance.** Candidate set = the 24 saved
`vanilla_ppo_iter_{00010..00240}.pt`. Per seed:
* `score_A` = honest rate on eval seed 1, at the checkpoint argmax'd on
  eval seed 0;
* `score_B` = honest rate on eval seed 0, at the checkpoint argmax'd on
  eval seed 1;
* seed score = `(score_A + score_B) / 2`.

100 scoring episodes per seed, **every episode used exactly once as a
scoring episode and never for the selection that chose its own
checkpoint** — no within-seed selection leakage in either direction, and
the full n is retained rather than halved. Ties → later iter, both
directions reported. Honest protocol immutable: cold entrance, greedy,
sticky 0.25, jitter ±16, 50 eps × eval seeds {0,1}, `--eval-rng
per-episode`, max_steps 1500, `rom_sha256` on every receipt.

**Winner's-curse budget.** Cross-fit removes the within-seed curse;
best-of-4 across seeds is still a max statistic carrying the measured
**0.05**. Reported alongside Θ as `Θ_adj = Θ − 0.05`. A PASS landing in
[0.80, 0.85) is flagged **"PASS within the measured curse"** and requires
a confirmation re-score of the winning seed's selected checkpoint on a
**third eval seed (es2, 50 fresh episodes, registered now)** before it may
be called PASS in any external claim. ~30 s of eval. This budgets the
curse without touching the numeral.

**Secondary — the actual ReDo effect. Registered now so it cannot be a
post-hoc rescue, and it cannot produce a PASS.**
`Δ = Θ − Θ_v27`, where `Θ_v27` is v27's four banked seeds re-scored under
the **identical** cross-fit estimator (§8 pays for this).
* `Δ ≥ +0.15` → "ReDo is a real lever on this stack" — a mechanism
  finding, reported as such, not a gate PASS.
* `Δ ≤ +0.05` → "ReDo is not a lever at the dose that fires."
* in between → indeterminate at n=4; say so.

The likeliest outcome is Θ in 0.50–0.65: **FAIL on the registered gate,
informative on the fork.** Fixing Δ in advance is what makes that outcome
worth the compute instead of another dead FAIL.

## 5. Interpretation — the DR's own fork, with its one amendment

Per the DR §6, used as written:

* **PASS (Θ ≥ 0.80)** → Hypothesis A (consolidation) CONFIRMED,
  Hypothesis B (48k budget) FALSIFIED. Post-hoc fine-tuning retired for
  this stack; all future runs use from-scratch curricula with embedded
  edge cases plus continuous plasticity regularization.
* **FAIL (Θ ≤ 0.767)** → the DR reads this as Hypothesis B CONFIRMED.

**Registered amendment to the DR's FAIL inference.** The DR's
"ReDo mathematically guarantees all 48k parameters were active" rests on
identity-preserving surgical recycling. On this architecture that
guarantee is measurably false: at tau 0.25 a firing event changes the
greedy argmax on ~19% of sampled states (agree 0.814). A FAIL is
therefore confounded with "ReDo at the minimum firing dose damaged a
healthy network." That confound is bounded, not eliminated, by the A4
identity abort and by Δ. **A v30 FAIL does not, on its own, confirm
Hypothesis B**, and any claim that it does is a fabrication. It licenses
exactly: "plasticity loss in Sokar's sense is not the large lever here."

## 7. Aborts

* **A1 — Phase M abort (~9 min, no training).** If the minimum dormancy
  score across all 20 iterations and both layers never drops below 0.25,
  the treatment cannot fire meaningfully. Escalate once to tau 0.30 per
  §2; if that also shows no sub-0.30 tail, **abort the arm before
  training** and bank: *"dormancy in Sokar's sense does not develop in a
  LayerNorm+SiLU trunk; the DR's mechanism has no substrate here."*
  Cheaper and stronger than a 7-hour FAIL.
* **A2 — arming abort (~10.6 min).** `cum_recycled == 0` at iter 25 →
  `RuntimeError`, whole 4-seed sequence aborts, **VOID**.
* **A3 — damage abort (~45 min).** After treatment seed 0 reaches iter
  100: if cumulative entrance rate < **0.10** *and* trailing rate <
  **0.10**, cancel seeds 1–3. v27's worst seed at iter 100 was 0.186
  cumulative / 0.23 trailing; the bar is below half of that on **both**
  axes, so it fires only on catastrophic degradation, never on seed
  noise. Verdict: "ReDo at tau 0.25 is destructive on this stack."
* **A4 — identity abort.** If median greedy-argmax `agree` over the first
  50 recycle events < **0.60**, the step is a partial reset, not a
  surgical intervention, and the arm would be uninterpretable. Abort and
  report. (Measured 0.814 at tau 0.25; 0.643 at tau 0.30 — live if §2
  escalates.)
* **A5 — wall clock.** 12.0 h hard. At the ceiling, stop and report what
  is banked.

1. The first **armed** execution of the DR's mandated intervention, at a

## 9. The confound I am accepting, and the control-arm ruling

**Named confound:** v27 changed the curriculum (merged 785-rung ladder
with the 27 recovery states) **and** armed ReDo together, so a ReDo-fires
arm compared against v27 is not automatically a clean single-variable
test.

**Ruling: no new control arm. `control_arm = false`.** The matched
tau-0.025 control **already exists with receipts.** Because ReDo was
provably inert in v27 — 0 recycles on every check of all four seeds — the
v27 arm *is* the ReDo-off control: identical merged ladder, identical
recipe, identical seeds 0–3, identical 250 iters, identical num_envs.
The inertness that voided v27 as a ReDo test is exactly what makes it a
valid ReDo-off control. Spending 7.2 h to re-derive a number already
banked would consume the entire remaining budget to reproduce a receipt.

The 1.5 h I *do* spend is the honest part of that ruling: backfilling
v27's 24-point × 2-eval-seed honest ladder so Δ is computed under the
**identical** cross-fit estimator on both arms. Comparing a new
cross-fit number against v27's rule-R 0.500 would be an estimator
mismatch, and fixing the estimator in advance is a condition of this
registration.

**Residual risks accepted and named:**
* **Not a paired test.** ReDo consumes numpy RNG at every firing
  (`np.random.choice`), so the treatment's rollout stream diverges from
  v27's at the first recycle. This is four independent draws against four
  independent draws, not a paired comparison.
* **Machine conditions differ** across two days; mitigated by the s/iter
  band above, flagged in the verdict if breached.
* **Protocol note, recorded so it is never used as a rescue.** The
  0.767 bar was measured at n=60, eval seed 0 only, 1 worker. Re-measured
  under *this* registration's protocol (100 pooled, 2 eval seeds) the
  banked control reads **0.68**
  (`runs/v27_readjudication_2026-08-27/readjudication.json`). **0.767
  does not move** — it is a numeral fixed pre-run, and moving it would be
  a fabricated result. The 0.68 figure is reported as context only and
  carries no verdict weight.

## 10. What n = 4 cannot conclude

Across the 8 banked runs the per-seed honest rate is **bimodal**: 2 of 8
seeds collapsed below 0.10 (v27 seed0 0.03, v28 seed2 0.09) and the other
6 sit in a tight band 0.46–0.67 (mean 0.527, sd ≈ 0.08). v27's four-seed
sd is 0.226 — almost entirely the one collapsed seed. **Max single seed
ever observed: 0.67.**

Therefore, with n = 4:

* Powered only for a **≥ +0.25** shift in the per-seed honest rate. A
  real but modest lever — +0.05 to +0.15, which is the size the recovery
  assay's own ceiling analysis (0.767 → 0.83–0.85) predicts is even
  available — is **invisible at this n**. A FAIL rules out "ReDo is a big
  lever"; it cannot rule out "ReDo is a small lever."
* PASS requires a seed 0.13 above the best of all 8 prior seeds. Absent a
  large effect, FAIL is near-certain by construction — which is what the
  gate is for, and why Δ (§4) exists to carry the informative signal.
* With a ~25% per-seed collapse rate, best-of-4 is a max statistic whose
  null already reaches 0.50. **A best-of-4 in the 0.50–0.65 range is
  indistinguishable from seed noise** and must be reported as such.
* n = 4 cannot separate "ReDo helped nothing" from "ReDo helped and hurt
  in equal measure." Only the mechanism read — per-seed trajectories,
  recycle-event counts, median agree, and the recovery-band advance
  telemetry — can speak to that, and it speaks qualitatively.
* n = 4 cannot confirm Hypothesis B. See §5's amendment.
