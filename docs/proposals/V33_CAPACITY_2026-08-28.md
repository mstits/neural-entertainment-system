## The seven §4.3 items

### 1. Construct-validity preflight

**Claim to demonstrate offline: the selection statistic is not a
deterministic readout of a quantity the treatment sets.** The treatment is
`tile_hidden_dim: 96 → 128`. The selection statistic is the split-sample
honest `clear_rate` — a count of flagpole/castle `episode_success` events
over 50 real episodes, produced by `scripts/eval_game.py` from emulator
rollouts. Width enters the eval path only through the checkpoint's own
weight shapes (loader infers dims); no term in `clear_rate`'s computation
reads a parameter count, a layer width, or any quantity the treatment sets.
**Demonstration owed before launch** (receipt:
`runs/v33_capacity/preflight_construct_validity.json`): evaluate one banked
48k checkpoint and one banked 72k checkpoint through the identical harness
and confirm the receipts differ only in `clear_rate`/episode fields, never
in any field derived from width. Contrast with the ReDo failure this item
generalizes: there the selection statistic (dormancy score) correlated
+0.77–0.94 with a quantity the operator set to a constant. Here the
statistic is an emulator outcome the treatment cannot write to.

### 2. The dose, and reachability at it

**`tile_hidden_dim: 128` (95,943 params), trunk 32 unchanged** — the third
point on the measured line 48,135 → 72,039 (+24k per step; this step +23,904,
matched within 0.5%). Single functional variable vs
`configs/mario_1_1_v28_seed{0..3}.yaml`, which was itself single-variable vs
v27. **Reachability demonstration owed before launch** (receipt:
`runs/v33_capacity/preflight_smoke.json`): a 5-iteration smoke at width 128
on the real recipe — network instantiates, trains, saves, resumes, and the
checkpoint's fc1 shape reads (128, 712). "The mechanism fires at the dose"
for a capacity treatment means the width actually trains; the arithmetic
that `redo_tau: 0.025` was unreachable existed before v27 and was not run —
this is the equivalent check, run.

### 3. The bar, with its provenance sentence

**PASS: Θ ≥ 0.80. FAIL: Θ ≤ 0.767.** Unchanged. Provenance, attached as
§4.3 requires: *0.767 is 46/60 at eval seed 0, shared-stream, one worker,
never measured under the two-seed per-episode protocol it gates.* It is
quoted with that sentence or not quoted. MARGINAL (0.767, 0.80) is reported
as MARGINAL and licenses nothing.

### 4. The power statement

Corrected per-seed values: v27 {0.110, 0.500, 0.460, 0.460}, v28 {0.640,
0.440, 0.580, 0.670} — pooled per-seed SD ≈ 0.16. At n = 4 seeds, best-of-4
against v28's 0.670, the design detects **Δ ≥ ≈ +0.25 at 80% power**; the
measured dose step was **+0.170**. **The design cannot reliably see the
effect the evidence predicts, and this registration says so rather than
hiding it.** Consequences, fixed now: (a) the primary deliverable is the
**dose-response point** (Θ_33 beside 0.500 and 0.670 on the params axis),
not the PASS/FAIL alone; (b) a FAIL with Θ ∈ (0.570, 0.767) is reported as
*"consistent with the measured slope, underpowered against the bar"* and
licenses NO further capacity campaign without a design change (more seeds
or a bar renegotiation registered first); (c) only Θ ≥ 0.80 or Θ ≤ 0.570
are decisive at this n.

### 5. The selection rule

Split-sample, per DIRECTION §4.1, the standing rule: per seed, argmax on
eval seed 0 scored on eval seed 1 and the mirror, mean of the two; ties →
later iteration; fixed 10-iteration grid (24 checkpoints, iters 10–240);
best-of-4 across seeds. Winner's-curse budget 0.05: Θ_adj = Θ − 0.05
reported beside Θ; a PASS in [0.80, 0.85) is *"PASS within the measured
curse"* and needs the es2 confirmation re-score (50 fresh episodes,
registered now) before any external claim.

### 6. The combination rule

There is exactly **one primary metric** (Θ as defined in item 5) and **one
registered secondary**: the dose-response slope read, defined now —
*improving* iff Θ_33 > 0.670 by any margin; *flat/regressing* otherwise; no
sub-metric may be added at adjudication. The v28 lesson (a parser written
after compute flipped the read 3/4 → 1/4) is closed by having nothing to
parse: no per-seed dose-response table is part of the verdict.

### 7. What a VOID licenses

**One sentence, fixed now: a VOID v33 licenses only the statement "the v33
campaign did not measure capacity at 96k," and neither confirms nor refutes
the capacity hypothesis, moves no bar, retires nothing, and does not
discharge or extend any standing prohibition.** VOID conditions: any seed
whose training dies before iter 240 (that seed is VOID; fewer than 4 scored
seeds → campaign VOID-UNDERPOWERED, no Θ, per-seed numbers banked as
mechanism receipts); a preflight failure (no launch); an eval receipt whose
`status != "ok"` at any grid point (re-run once; still bad → that seed
VOID).

## Protocol

Four seeds, `configs/mario_1_1_v33_capacity_seed{0..3}.yaml`, each a
two-line diff from its v28 counterpart (`name`, `tile_hidden_dim: 128`).
250 iterations, sequential, `--no-resume`, receipts under
`runs/v33_capacity/`. After all four: the identical F0/v27-ladder eval job
(24 checkpoints × 2 eval seeds × 4 seeds = 192 evals, the committed driver
pattern), then Θ per item 5. Honest protocol immutable: cold entrance,
greedy, sticky 0.25, jitter ±16, 50 eps × eval seeds {0,1}, per-episode
RNG, max_steps 1500. Budget ceiling: **16 h wall-clock** for training + 1.5 h
eval; exceeded → remaining seeds VOID, not rushed. ReDo stays at its
schema-default inert setting in all four configs — v33 is single-variable
and the plasticity prohibition (nothing in v27–v32 citable either way)
stands untouched.

## ADDENDUM 1 (2026-08-28, ~2.2 h into compute) — the configs violated this registration's own protocol sentence, and the arm deadline caught it

The Protocol section above states "ReDo stays at its schema-default inert
setting in all four configs." The minted configs, two-line diffs from their
v28 counterparts, silently inherited v28's `redo_enabled: true` at the inert
`tau 0.025` — armed-and-inert, the exact state the v32-era arming deadline
(`_REDO_ARM_DEADLINE_ITERS = 40`, added AFTER v28 ran) exists to refuse. All
four seeds VOIDed at iteration 40 across all six supervisor restarts,
~33 min each, ~2.2 h total. **The guard worked exactly as built; the
registration asserted the configs' state without reading the configs.**

Fix: `redo_enabled: false` in all four, conforming the configs to the
sentence already registered. No numeral moves. Single-variable status vs
v28 is unaffected in the only sense that matters — v28's ReDo fired zero
times (proven, ~2,000 checks), so armed-inert and disabled are numerically
identical training paths; the difference is that one of them is now
refused at launch by a guard that did not exist when v28 ran.

Attempt-1 artifacts are quarantined as `*.attempt1.log`; the crashed
partial checkpoint dirs and any partial ladder evals were deleted before
relaunch so no stale iter-10..40 checkpoint from the VOID attempt can leak
into the eval grid.
