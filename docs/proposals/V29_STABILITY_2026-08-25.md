# v29 — stability experiment: critic relief against peak instability

Registered 2026-08-25, before any v29 training ran, as the sequel the
v28 verdict handed the loop. v28's own "Secondary finding: peak
instability reproduces a THIRD time, and more starkly than ever"
(`docs/proposals/V28_CAPACITY_2026-08-25.md`) recorded the phenomenon
but had no registration to hand it to. The forensics campaign
(`docs/research/PEAK_INSTABILITY_FORENSICS_2026-08-25.md`, 18 analyses,
8 runs, zero new training compute) ranked the surviving candidates.
**This document tests the top-ranked one, and only that one.**

## Trigger condition — read this before spending anything

**This document is a design, not a launch order.** Three separate acts
are required before the 4-seed budget is spent, in this order, and each
one can stop the experiment:

1. **F0** (the mandatory zero-training-compute prerequisite) completes
   and produces the corrected honest peak ladder.
2. **F1** (the cheap mechanism falsifier) returns LIVE, not DEAD and not
   INCONCLUSIVE.
3. **F2** (the viability preflight, ~30 min of training) returns PASS.

If F1 returns DEAD, the top-ranked mechanism is refuted before any real
budget is spent and **this experiment does not run at all** — the
registered fallback (V30, below) takes the floor instead. If F1 returns
INCONCLUSIVE, that is *also* not a launch: it escalates to the owner
with the table, and an ambiguous preflight may not be read as a green
light. This ordering is not decoration. It is this project's binding
rule — cheap premise-falsifiers before big workflows
(`feedback_megaworkflow_sequencing_correction`) — and the last two
months of void history (2-1 attempt 1 on the wrong ladder, Phase 3's
frozen actor, the options 1e12 sentinel) is what it is paying for.

## The registered question

All eight completed from-scratch runs — v27 (48k params × 4 seeds) and
v28 (72k × 4 seeds), 250 iterations each — peak early and decay to
near-zero. Under the honest protocol (cold entrance, greedy, sticky
0.25, jitter ±16, 100 episodes pooled over eval seeds {0,1}) the
best-of-4 on the **preserved peak** artifact is 0.530 (v27) and 0.670
(v28); the best-of-4 on the **final iter-240** artifact is **0.020**
(v27) and **0.050** (v28). Preserve-on-peak is the only reason either
experiment has a number.

The forensics campaign closed ten mechanisms with evidence (dormancy,
action collapse, network freezing, curriculum non-stationarity, RND
decay, reward-mix shift, GAE horizon drift, training-reward
specialization, feature-rank collapse, winner's curse on the honest
peak), **falsified the campaign's own former top candidate** (decode /
argmax degradation), and demoted entropy collapse from prime suspect to
lagging readout. Three candidates remain live. This experiment asks the
question the highest-ranked one poses:

> **Is critic degradation a causal driver of peak instability, such that
> relieving the critic's grip on the shared trunk lets a run HOLD its
> capability to iter 240 instead of erasing it?**

Note the shape of the question. It is not "can we get a higher number."
It is "can a run keep what it earned." §"The gate" below is built around
that distinction and refuses to score the two the same way.

## What "top-ranked" honestly means here

Stated up front, because the forensics document itself insists on it and
a registration that quietly upgrades its premise is worse than one that
never had it:

- **No mechanism is established.** Rank 1 is the best-supported of three
  live candidates, not a confirmed cause.
- The critic evidence is a **whole-run correlation at N=8**: Spearman
  ρ = −0.857 between median `ppo_value_loss` and honest peak, surviving
  every leave-one-out. But it was selected from a scan of ~10 fields
  with no multiple-comparison correction; a Šidák threshold at 10
  comparisons wants |ρ| ≈ 0.88, so it lands *just short* even at its
  best.
- The campaign's adversarial pass rated the originating analysis
  **WEAKENED**, and the base rate is unflattering: of 14 recorded
  verdicts, **2 SURVIVES and 12 WEAKENED — and both SURVIVES verdicts
  are on analyses that returned nulls.** Every analysis proposing a
  positive mechanism came back WEAKENED. This one included.
- **The central ambiguity is unresolved on disk.** Raw Huber value loss
  conflates "the targets got harder to fit" with "the fit got worse." A
  dying policy produces noisier, more bimodal returns *purely
  mechanically*, which inflates value loss with no critic pathology at
  all. Nothing in the eight runs separates these.

That last bullet is why F1 exists and why it comes first. It is the
cheap falsifier, and it targets exactly the ambiguity that would
otherwise make a V29 FAIL uninterpretable.

## Single variable: `value_coef` 0.25 → 0.05, nothing else

`value_coef` is an ordinary schema-legal config key. Verified against
the tree, not assumed:

| fact | location |
|---|---|
| in the `reinforce` allow-list | `src/training/config_schema.py:75` |
| read once at trainer construction | `src/training/trainer.py:911` (`float(rl_cfg.get("value_coef", 0.5))`) |
| applied in the loss | `src/training/ppo.py:190` (`loss = policy_loss + value_coef * value_loss - entropy_coef * entropy_for_loss`) |
| applied in the updater path | `src/training/ppo_updater.py:549` (`_c_loss = t.value_coef * _vloss3`) |
| applied in the trainer's own path | `src/training/trainer.py:4658` |
| recorded in the run manifest | `src/training/run_manifest.py:28` |
| written explicitly in every v28 seed config | `configs/mario_1_1_v28_seed{0..3}.yaml` → `value_coef: 0.25` |

**There is no code to change.** One key, one value, four files. This is
the same "config-and-data only" discipline v28 earned its
interpretability from, and it is the reason a v29 result can be read
seed-for-seed against v28's already-banked runs.

**Why this is the dose.** The forensics measured the value term at
**99.3–99.5% of combined gradient magnitude** (median over all 2,000
metrics rows, both widths, all eight runs) — a consequence of the shared
trunk in `TilePolicyNetwork`, where actor and critic heads pull on the
same 68k-parameter body. 0.25 → 0.05 is a 5× reduction in the value
term's weight in the loss, and it is the exact intervention the
forensics' own Rank-1 falsifiable prediction named ("dropping
`value_coef` from 0.25 to ~0.05"). Registering a smaller dose would risk
a null indistinguishable from noise at four seeds; registering a larger
one (0.0) would remove the critic entirely and stop being an experiment
about advantage quality.

**Why `value_coef` alone, not the alternatives.** Three ways exist to
relieve critic pressure on this architecture:

| Candidate | Change | Code touched | Confounds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 (chosen)** | `value_coef` 0.25 → 0.05 | none — config only | relative reweighting (see the registered limitation below) |
| 2 | detach the critic head onto its own MLP | `tile_policy.py` save/load contract, `build_tile_policy_from_checkpoint`, ARCH_VERSION bump | adds ~2–3k params → confounds with capacity, which v28 just spent 8 h measuring |
| 3 | separate critic learning rate | new optimizer param group; `_build_ppo_optimizer` change | two effective knobs (lr *and* coefficient) in one arm |

Candidate 2 is the mechanistically cleanest test of "shared-trunk
contamination" and is named here as the natural graduated follow-up —
**not run now, not budgeted now** — because it changes the checkpoint
contract and adds a second architecture path, two more places for a
silent-mechanism void to hide, for a question this experiment is not
asking. Candidate 3 collapses to Candidate 1 under Adam's per-parameter
normalization for the head itself but not for the shared trunk, so it is
strictly more confounded, not less. **Candidate 1 is registered as the
primary and only arm.**

`grep -rni "explained_var\|explained variance" src/ scripts/` returns

a second variable; it touches no training path.

every V29 statistic is peak-relative and a peak that is wrong by 40
iterations makes them all wrong.

**What runs.** The full honest protocol on **every 10-iter checkpoint**
already on disk — 25 per run — for the four v28 control runs
(**mandatory**: they supply the retention denominators) and the four v27
runs (**strongly recommended**, +1.4 h, closes the forensics' 3-run

**F0 has no pass/fail.** It is a measurement prerequisite. It cannot
kill the hypothesis; it can only stop V29 from being scored against
numbers that are wrong.

## F1 — the cheap mechanism falsifier (zero training compute) — **THIS IS THE KILL SWITCH**

**The premise F1 tests.** Rank 1 claims the critic *degrades*. The only
evidence on disk is that raw Huber `ppo_value_loss` rises — which is
equally consistent with the critic being fine and the *returns* becoming
harder to predict as the policy sharpens and outcomes go bimodal. If
that second reading is right, the value-loss rise is a symptom of the
collapse rather than a cause of it, `value_coef` is not a lever, and
**the 8-hour V29 spend is wasted before it starts.**

**The instrument.** `scripts/critic_explained_variance.py` (new; the
quantity exists nowhere in the tree). For a checkpoint `t` and a batch
of states with targets `R`:

```
EV = 1 - Var(R - V_t(s)) / Var(R)
```

Two variants, and the contrast between them is the whole point:

- **`EV_onpolicy(t)`** — checkpoint `t`'s critic on a fresh rollout
  collected by checkpoint `t`'s own policy. Confounded by design: a
  collapsing policy visits a different state distribution.
- **`EV_fixed(t)`** — checkpoint `t`'s critic on a **frozen reference
  batch**, collected **once** per run from that run's corrected-peak
  checkpoint (16,384 states subsampled uniformly from one
  `rollout_steps=1024 × num_envs=60` rollout; ~47 MB fp32 per run).
  Targets are **Monte-Carlo discounted returns to episode end**, not GAE
  — states whose episode does not terminate inside the rollout are
  dropped. **This is deliberate: MC targets carry no value-function
  bootstrap, so the frozen batch cannot favor the critic that collected
  it.**

`EV_fixed` is a fixed-target-set fit-quality measurement. It is exactly
the discriminator the forensics said was missing.

**Grid.** Per run, checkpoints {P−20, P−10, P, P+10, P+20, P+40, P+60} ∩
{available}, where `P` is the corrected peak from F0. All 8 runs.

**Cost.** ~56 forward-only rollouts of 61,440 env steps, no gradients.
Against v28's measured ~29 s per full training iteration (rollout +
gradients), a rollout-only pass is well under that: **≲ 45 min**, plus
authoring the instrument.

**Pre-registered three-way kill rule.** Let
`Δ_fixed = EV_fixed(last available grid point) − EV_fixed(P)`.

| outcome | condition | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **LIVE** | `Δ_fixed ≤ −0.15` in **≥ 5 of 8** runs | critic fit genuinely degrades on a fixed target set. Proceed to F2. |
| **DEAD** | `Δ_fixed > −0.10` in **≥ 5 of 8** runs | critic fit **held** while behavior collapsed. The value-loss rise is target noise. **Rank 1 is refuted and V29 does not launch.** Fall through to V30. |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | anything else (split field, or a majority in the −0.15…−0.10 band) | **not a launch.** Escalate to the owner with the per-run table. An ambiguous preflight may not be read as a green light. |

**Registered secondary read** (non-gating, one line of the same output):
if `EV_onpolicy` declines sharply while `EV_fixed` holds, that is the
distribution-shift signature and it is *itself* the answer to the
forensics' central ambiguity — worth banking regardless of which branch
fires.

**Why this rule is honest.** The DEAD branch is reachable and the design
does not hedge against it: `Δ_fixed > −0.10` in 5 of 8 runs kills the
document's own premise for the price of an afternoon's eval. That is the
point of putting it first.

## F2 — viability preflight (~30 min of training)

**The premise F2 tests.** `value_coef: 0.05` might sit *below the
learning floor*: with the critic's weight cut 5×, advantage estimates
may be too poor for the policy to walk the reverse curriculum at all. A
4-seed FAIL caused by "it never learned anything" would be
uninterpretable — it would not be evidence against critic relief, just
evidence that this dose does not train.

**What runs.** One seed (**seed 3**, the strongest v28 seed and the one
holding the 0.670 headline), the exact V29 seed-3 config, **60
iterations only**:

```
.venv/bin/python scripts/train_game.py --game mario \
  --profile configs/mario_1_1_v29_seed3.yaml \
  --iters 60 --seed 3 --no-resume --strict-config \
  2>&1 | tee runs/v29_stability/preflight/f2_seed3_60iter.log
```

**Cost.** v28's four seeds ran sequentially with log-start timestamps
14:22 → 16:03 → 18:04 → 20:04, i.e. **~2 h per 250-iter seed**. 60 iters
≈ **~30 min**, one sixteenth of the full budget.

**Pre-registered PASS conditions — all three required.**

| # | condition | grounding |
|---|---|---|
| a | the ladder reaches the true entrance (`tau = 0`) **by iter 40** | all 8 predecessor runs reached it at iters **22–29**; +11 iterations of margin |
| b | at least one `winners/best.json` write with `entrance_trailing_rate ≥ 0.50` by iter 60 | all 8 predecessors saturate this metric at 0.867–1.000; 0.50 is a floor, not a match |
| c | honest pooled greedy at the iter-60 checkpoint **≥ 0.15** (n=100, eval seeds {0,1}, harness pinned) | v28 seed3's honest at iter 50 was 0.600 and seed0's 0.200 (n=30 decay curve); 0.15 is a non-starter test, not a performance test |

**Any of (a), (b), (c) failing → do not spend the 4-seed budget at
0.05.** The registered response is a single AMENDMENT to this document
re-registering the dose at **`value_coef: 0.12`** (still exactly one key,
still a single variable) with F2 re-run from scratch at that value. Two
consecutive F2 failures close the `value_coef` lever entirely and hand
the floor to V30.

**F2 is not a preview of the gate.** It cannot PASS the experiment and
its numbers are never reported as a result. It is a floor check.

## Registered limitation: the reweighting confound

Stated in advance so it cannot be discovered conveniently afterward, in
the same spirit as v28's "Why capacity-only" section.

The PPO objective is `loss = policy_loss + value_coef * value_loss -
entropy_coef * entropy`. Cutting `value_coef` 5× does not only relieve
the critic — **it also raises the policy and entropy terms' weight
*relative to* the value term by 5×.** Under Adam's per-parameter
normalization this is not a clean global rescale, but it is not nothing
either, and it means a V29 PASS is ambiguous between two channels:

- **(i) critic relief** — the registered hypothesis: less critic
  gradient on the shared trunk preserves the representation.
- **(ii) relative amplification** of the policy-gradient and entropy
  terms — a different mechanism wearing the same knob.

**This is not separable by this design.** The alternative that would
separate it — scaling `lr` down by a matched factor to hold total
gradient magnitude fixed — is a second variable, which is exactly what
"single variable versus the v28 configs" forbids.

Two things follow, both registered:

1. **The entropy trajectory is a designated discriminating read** (read
   #4 below). If V29 retains capability while entropy still collapses
   monotonically below 0.10, channel (ii) via the entropy term is
   largely excluded and channel (i) is the better explanation. If
   entropy stops collapsing, the causal chain is genuinely ambiguous and
   the reading-rule table says so rather than claiming the win.
2. **The lr-matched control is pre-named as V30-C**, to be run only if
   V29 PASSes — you do not spend a disambiguation budget on an
   intervention that did not work.

# The gate

**This is the part that separates a stability fix from a higher peak,
and it is the reason this document is not a v28 clone.**

v28's gate scored `best-of-4` over each seed's *better of two*
checkpoints — the preserved peak and the final. Under that rule a run
that peaks 0.75 and collapses to 0.00 and a run that peaks 0.67 and
holds 0.67 **score identically**. That is precisely the confusion this
experiment exists to resolve, so the selection rule changes. It is the
only thing about the gate that changes.

## Definitions

For each seed `i`, computed from the V29 honest ladder (every 10-iter
checkpoint, n=100 pooled, harness pinned — the same ladder F0 builds for
the control):

```
P_i  = pooled honest greedy at the LADDER-ARGMAX checkpoint   (ties -> later iter)
F_i  = pooled honest greedy at iter 240 (the FINAL checkpoint)
R_i  = F_i / P_i                    -- defined ONLY when P_i >= 0.20
L_i  = count of consecutive ladder points from the argmax onward
       with honest >= 0.7 * P_i     -- the plateau length, in iterations
```

Three properties of these definitions, registered because each one
blocks a way of gaming the gate:

- **`P_i` is measured, never the `winners/best.json` proxy.** The
  forensics showed that proxy mis-selects the honest maximum in 4 of 5
  runs probed, by up to 4×. `winners/best.json` may be cited as
  telemetry and may never be used as `P_i`.
- **`P_i ≥ F_i` by construction** (iter 240 is on the ladder), so
  `R_i ∈ [0, 1]`, and `F_i ≥ 0.40` cannot be reached by a run that was
  never capable.
- **`R_i` is undefined when `P_i < 0.20`, and an undefined `R_i` can
  never satisfy a gate condition.** This is not a technicality: the
  control's own highest retention ratio is v27 seed0's 0.500, which is
  2/100 divided by 4/100 — a flat, near-zero run masquerading as perfect
  retention. Without this clause the degenerate shape passes.

Note also that `P_i` is itself a maximum over 25 measurements, so it
carries a small upward selection bias — which makes `R_i` biased
**downward**. That is the conservative direction for a PASS claim and is
left uncorrected. (The forensics measured the honest peak's neighbour
ratio at median 0.965, so the bias is small.) A robustness read using
`P'_i` = median of the three highest ladder points is reported alongside
but does not gate.

## Pre-registered bars

The experiment's number is best-of-4 on the **final** checkpoint,
`F4 = max_i F_i`, with a retention side-condition on the seed that
supplies it.

- **PASS** — some seed has **`F_i ≥ 0.40` AND `R_i ≥ 0.60`.**
  Peak instability is *fixed*, not dodged: the run held ≥ 60% of its
  measured maximum to iter 240 and the surviving artifact clears 0.40
  cold. Grounding: **0.40 is 8× the best final-checkpoint number this
  line has ever produced** (v28's 0.050) and above half of v28's best
  measured peak (0.670/2 = 0.335). **0.60 retention** is against a
  control field whose non-degenerate retention ratios are
  0.000/0.000/0.000/0.217.
- **MARGINAL-STABILITY** — some seed has **`R_i ≥ 0.60` with
  `F_i ∈ [0.20, 0.40)`.** Retention improved materially; capability fell
  short. **This is registered in advance as the more scientifically
  valuable of the two marginals** and must be reported as a positive
  mechanism result, not as a near-miss: it says the collapse is
  *interruptible*, which nothing in eight runs has shown.
- **MARGINAL-PEAK** — some seed has **`F_i ≥ 0.40`** but **no seed
  reaches `R_i ≥ 0.60`.** The artifact got better; the decay is intact.
  **Explicitly NOT a stability result** and must not be reported as one,
  however good the headline number looks.
- **FAIL** — no seed reaches `F_i ≥ 0.20`; **or** the only seeds with
  `R_i ≥ 0.60` have `P_i < 0.20` (the degenerate flat-low shape).

**Why the primary statistic is the final checkpoint and not the peak.**
Because scoring the final checkpoint *is* the design decision that
answers the constraint. A run that peaks 0.75 and collapses scores
`F ≈ 0.00` and FAILs. A run that peaks 0.67 and holds scores `F = 0.67`
and PASSes. The retention ratio is then the guard against the one
remaining degenerate case — a flat mediocre run that "retains" 0.15 —
and the mechanism read that says *whether* the shape changed rather than
the level.

**Preserve-on-peak stays mandatory** (`winners/best.pt` keeps being
written) — it is how eight predecessor experiments got numbers at all,
and V29 must not become the one run that discards its own best artifact
to make a point. But **the preserved peak does not enter the gate.** It
is reported as `P_i` context alongside.

**The best-outcome edge case.** If a seed's ladder-argmax *is* iter 240
— the run never turned over — then `R_i = 1.0` and `F_i = P_i`. That is
the strongest possible result and PASSes on `F_i ≥ 0.40` alone. Say so
in the writeup if it happens; do not bury it inside a ratio of 1.0.

## Registered non-gate mechanism reads

1. **Critic gradient share.** Fraction of combined gradient magnitude
   contributed by the value term, per iteration, against the control's
   99.3–99.5%. **If this does not fall materially, the knob did not take
   and the run is VOID, not FAIL** (V3).
2. **In-run `EV_fixed` trajectory.** With the explained-variance scalar
   now in the metrics sink (F0), does critic fit on a fixed target set
   hold through iter 240 in V29 where it fell in v28? This is the
   mechanism half of the reading rule.
3. **Plateau length `L_i`, seed-paired against v28's** (from F0's
   control ladder). Prediction registered below.
4. **Entropy trajectory, seed-paired.** The designated discriminator for
   the reweighting confound. Report entropy at iters 20/60/120/180/240
   for each V29 seed against its v28 counterpart. *(v28 seed3 reference:
   1.4656 / 0.7318 / 0.2910 / 0.0796 / 0.0716.)*
5. **Ladder telemetry** — iters-to-entrance, rungs/100 iters,
   recovery-band trailing rung rate — seed-paired against v28, the same
   reads v28 ran against v27, so the three experiments remain directly
   comparable.
6. **ReDo dormancy/recycle counts.** Expected: identically zero, as in
   all 2,000 predecessor checks. A non-zero count at reduced
   `value_coef` would be a genuinely new fact about the mechanism and
   gets its own line in the writeup.

## The falsifiable prediction

Registered before any V29 compute runs so it cannot be fitted
afterwards:

> **Critic-degradation-is-causal** predicts that relieving the critic
> produces a **retention effect, not merely a level effect**:
> specifically, in **at least 3 of the 4 paired seeds**, the plateau
> length `L_i` (read #3) exceeds that same seed's v28 `L_i` by **≥ 30
> iterations**, AND the in-run `EV_fixed` trajectory (read #2) stays
> within 0.10 of its own peak value through iter 240 — **regardless of
> whether the honest gate itself clears `F4 ≥ 0.40`.** That is the
> signature: the *shape* changes even if the *level* falls short.
>
> **Critic-degradation-is-symptomatic** predicts the opposite: plateau
> lengths flat or mixed against v28, and `EV_fixed` declining through
> the crash exactly as it did at `value_coef = 0.25`, despite the 5×
> reduction. Under that outcome the value-loss correlation was a
> downstream reading of a collapse driven elsewhere, `value_coef` is not
> a lever, and the fork redirects to Rank 2 (the Adam second-moment
> ratchet on the output-head biases) rather than to a different critic
> dose.

## Reading rule — (gate outcome) × (mechanism direction) → verdict

Made concrete so no part of it gets eyeballed after the fact. The
mechanism axis is read #2, the in-run `EV_fixed` trajectory through iter
240, with plateau length (read #3) as the tiebreak within a row.

| Gate | `EV_fixed` through iter 240 | Verdict text |
|---|---|---|
| **PASS** | holds (Δ > −0.10 from its own peak) | **Critic degradation CONFIRMED as a causal driver of peak instability**, and `value_coef` is a real stability lever. Rank 1 is promoted from candidate to established. Next questions are dose and generality (other levels, other widths), not mechanism. Run V30-C (lr-matched control) to close the reweighting confound. |
| **PASS** | declines like v28's | Stability was fixed but **not via critic fit** — the knob acted through the reweighting channel (ii). Rank 1 is **not** confirmed; the result is real and its mechanism is misattributed if reported as critic relief. V30-C (lr-matched control) becomes mandatory, not optional. |
| **MARGINAL-STABILITY** | holds | **Critic relief is a real, partial stability lever.** The collapse is interruptible — the first such demonstration in this line. Dose or a co-mechanism is missing: V30 = graduated dose (0.12) or Rank-1 + Rank-2 co-intervention, registered fresh. |
| **MARGINAL-STABILITY** | declines | Retention improved while critic fit did not: **Rank 1 is not the mechanism**, but something in this knob is. V30-C first, to identify which channel acted, before any further dose work. |
| **MARGINAL-PEAK** | either | Higher artifact, decay intact. **Not a stability result and may not be reported as one.** Rank 1 unsupported as a stability mechanism; the +peak effect is banked as a capability data point only. Fork goes to V30 (Rank 2). |
| **FAIL** | declines like v28's | Rank 1 survives as a **correlate** and is refuted as an **intervention target at this dose**. Registered response: V30 = Rank 2 (optimizer-moment reset), which the forensics call the cleanest discriminator and which predicts an effect where Ranks 1 and 3 predict none. |
| **FAIL** | **holds** (critic fit maintained, behavior still collapsed) | **Rank 1 is DEAD.** Critic fit was preserved and the collapse happened anyway — the strongest refutation this design can produce, and it closes the top-ranked candidate outright. Fork to Rank 2; if Rank 2 also fails, the standing conclusion is the forensics' own: **the mechanism is something none of the 18 analyses proposed**, and that outcome is registered here in advance so it cannot be explained away later. |

**Registered in advance: "no arm works" is a permitted and meaningful
outcome.** Given that this campaign's record is 2 SURVIVES (both nulls)
against 12 WEAKENED, the possibility that Rank 1, Rank 2 and Rank 3 are
*all* wrong is live. If V29 FAILs and V30 FAILs, the correct report is
"three candidate mechanisms are now closed and the cause is
unidentified" — not a fourth reanalysis of the same eight logs.

## VOID conditions

A VOID is "the experiment never happened"; no verdict may be read.

- **V0 — preflight-conditional launch.** Spending the 4-seed budget
  without **F0 complete**, **F1 recorded LIVE**, and **F2 recorded PASS**
  — each with its receipt on disk — is itself a VOID. An INCONCLUSIVE
  F1 is not a launch.
- **V1 — single-variable diff check.** Machine diff of all four
  `configs/mario_1_1_v29_seed{N}.yaml` against
  `configs/mario_1_1_v28_seed{N}.yaml`: VOID if any key other than
  `name`, `description`, `value_coef` differs. **Receipt
  `runs/v29_stability/config_diff.json` must actually be written** —
  v28's identically-registered receipt was never produced, and a
  registered receipt path that is never written is a gap even when the
  check itself was honest.
- **V2 — parameter-count receipt.** The seed-0 startup log must print
  `[policy] class=TilePolicyNetwork params=72039 (hidden=96,
  trunk/gru=32, features=712, actions=6)` exactly — the **same** number
  as v28. V29 is not a capacity experiment; any other value means a
  width drifted and the single-variable claim is broken.
- **V3 — mechanism-armed-and-not-inert.** Two parts. (a) `run_manifest.json`
  for every seed must record `value_coef: 0.05` (the field is already
  captured — `src/training/run_manifest.py:28`). (b) Read #1, the critic
  gradient share, must **fall materially** from the control's 99.3–99.5%.
  A run where the manifest says 0.05 but the gradient share is unchanged
  is mechanism-armed-but-inert — the exact class that has voided prior
  work here — and is VOID, not FAIL.
- **V4 — ReDo unchanged and armed.** `[redo] ENABLED tau=0.025
  every_iters=1 scope=fc1,fc2 sample=4096 reset_moments=true` present in
  all four logs, `[redo] disabled` in none. Continued inertness (0
  recycles) is the expected baseline and is **not** a VOID.
- **V5 — wrong-ladder guard.** Any seed whose `[backward] ENABLED` line
  does not name `checkpoints/backward_states/1-1-v27` with the full
  785-entry count is VOID for that seed (the class that voided 2-1
  attempt 1).
- **V6 — ladder liveness, per seed.** A seed whose tau has advanced 0
  rungs by iter 150 is VOID-machinery for that seed. If all four void
  this way the experiment is VOID, not FAIL.
- **V7 — eval lineage and harness pinning.** Every gate and ladder
  receipt must record `start_state:
  runs/live_show/smb_4_4_micro/entrance_start.state`, `sticky_prob:
  0.25`, `start_jitter: 16`, `action_select: greedy`, `n_episodes: 50`,
  **`eval_workers: 8`** and **`eval_rng: per-episode`**. Any receipt
  missing or disagreeing on a pinned field is discarded, not reported.
  This closes the cross-cohort harness confound the forensics found.
- **V8 — git sha uniformity within the arm.** All four V29 seeds must
  record the **same** `git_sha` in `winners/best.json` and the run
  manifest. The eight predecessor runs span **six distinct shas**, a
  confound the campaign never checked; V29 does not repeat it. Differing
  shas VOID the cross-seed pooling (not necessarily the individual
  seeds). The control's own six shas remain a stated limitation of the
  seed-paired comparison and must be repeated in the writeup.
- **V9 — retention denominators must exist.** No retention number may be
  reported for any seed pair whose control-side `P_i` was not measured
  by F0. `winners/best.json` is not an acceptable substitute (§"The
  gate"). VOIDs the specific read, not the experiment.
- **V10 — no mid-run knob edits.** `value_coef` is a constant for the
  whole run; there is no schedule and none may be added. Any run where
  the manifest and the config disagree, or where a CLI override altered
  a recipe key, is VOID for that seed — the class that produced the
  banked control's unrecoverable `num_envs: 24`.

**Not a VOID, registered explicitly:** a seed that trains cleanly but
never exceeds `P_i = 0.20`. That is a per-seed no-capability result and
is reported as such. Only an all-four-seeds version of it triggers
reconsideration of the dose, and even then via AMENDMENT, not a silent
relaunch.

## What this document does not do

It does not consult DR — the ranking work is already done and recorded
with its own adversarial verification; the only judgment calls here
(which candidate, which dose, which control, which gate shape) are
mechanical choices with stated rationales, not design questions needing
external adjudication. It does not touch
`scripts/merge_recovery_ladder.py`, `recovery_assay.py`, or
`mine_recovery_tapes.py` — the 785-rung ladder is reused byte-for-byte
from v27/v28, no re-mining, no re-merging. It does not arm any of the
existing stability machinery (entropy guard, entropy floor, KL anchor,
SIL, SAM); each would be a second variable. It does not change the
architecture, the width, the reward, the curriculum, the budget, or the
protocol. And it does not decide anything about 1-2/1-3/1-4 — those
levels have their own banked controls and their own measured assay
ceilings (1-2's trainable slice is 3/16 against 1-1's 3/9) and would
need their own registration derived from their own numbers.

Finally, and most importantly: **it does not promise that any of this
works.** The top-ranked candidate here is a WEAKENED correlation at N=8
that lands just short of a multiple-comparison-corrected threshold, and
the cheapest thing in this document is the switch designed to kill it.
