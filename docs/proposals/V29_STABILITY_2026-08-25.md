# v29 — the peak-instability experiment (retention, not peak height)

Registered 2026-08-25/26, before any v29 training ran, as the successor
the v28 FAIL verdict and the forensics campaign that followed it hand
the loop. The filename carries the campaign date of its parent
(`docs/research/PEAK_INSTABILITY_FORENSICS_2026-08-25.md`); the two were
written in one continuous session.

v28's verdict named "Candidate 2 or a further width step" as the natural
v29 **as a data point, not a foregone conclusion** — its own words. This
document does not take that option. The forensics campaign that ran
after that verdict changed what the next experiment should be, and this
registration says why in §2 rather than quietly substituting a different
question.

## Trigger condition — read this before spending anything

**This document is a design, not a launch order**, and it is
conditional twice over.

1. It fires only if the owner approves the redirect in §2 — away from
   the width step v28's verdict named, toward the collapse the forensics
   campaign says is the larger effect.
2. **Even with that approval, the 4-seed budget is not authorised until
   Preflight P1 passes.** P1 (§6) costs zero training compute and can
   kill the hypothesis outright. If P1 returns the kill result, this

Whoever reads P1's result makes the launch call. The existence of this
document is not that call.

## 1. The registered question

Every one of the 8 completed from-scratch runs in this line — v27
(48.1k params × 4 seeds) and v28 (72.0k params × 4 seeds), 250
iterations each — peaks in the first quarter to half of its budget and
then loses essentially all of its capability:

| run | peak iter | % through | honest @ peak | honest @ iter 240 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v27 s0 | 60 | 24% | 0.040 | 0.020 |
| v27 s1 | 50 | 20% | 0.290 | 0.020 |
| v27 s2 | 90 | 36% | 0.530 | 0.000 |
| v27 s3 | 60 | 24% | 0.170 | 0.010 |
| v28 s0 | 70 | 28% | 0.450 | 0.000 |
| v28 s1 | 60 | 24% | 0.230 | 0.050 |
| v28 s2 | 120 | 48% | 0.370 | 0.000 |
| v28 s3 | 90 | 36% | 0.670 | 0.000 |

Honest protocol throughout: cold entrance from
`runs/live_show/smb_4_4_micro/entrance_start.state`, greedy, sticky
0.25, jitter ±16, 50 episodes × eval seeds {0,1} = 100 pooled, max-steps
1500, `eval_workers 8`, `eval_rng per-episode`. Peak iterations are
re-derived from `checkpoints/<run>/winners/best.json` (`source_iter`),
**not** from the `[backward]` log line — that line is a lower bound, per
v28's own instrumentation-gap #1.

**Preserve-on-peak is the only reason any of these experiments has a
number.** Scored on final checkpoints alone, v27's best-of-4 would be
0.020 and v28's would be 0.050 instead of 0.670.

v27 asked about consolidation. v28 asked about capacity. **v29 asks
about the collapse itself: can the back half of the run be made to hold
what the front half found?**

## 2. Why this and not the width step v28's verdict named

v28's registered reading rule selected the row "capacity is a *real,
partial* lever" and named a further width step as the natural v29. Three
things argue for redirecting, and they are stated here so the redirect
is a decision on the record rather than a drift:

1. **The effect sizes are not comparable.** The width step bought
   +0.14 at the gate (best-of-4 0.530 → 0.670), on a 2-up/2-down seed
   field that best-of-4 structurally flatters. The collapse destroys
   **100% of capability in 6 of 8 runs and ≥78% in the other two.** A
   lever that recovers even half of what the back half throws away
   dominates another 24k parameters.
2. **The collapse is now known to be indifferent to width.** It
   reproduced at 48k and at 72k, identically in shape, and v28's own
   verdict wrote: "Adding capacity did not mitigate it. If anything the
   taller peaks made the fall further." Another width step tests a lever
   already measured not to touch this.
3. **The forensics campaign closed six candidate mechanisms and
   produced one ranked survivor with a concrete, zero-code intervention
   available.** That is a cheaper and more decisive next data point than
   a fifth capacity number.

This does not retract v28's verdict. Capacity remains a real partial
lever and the balanced widen (`hidden_dim` 64→80 + `trunk_dim` 32→40)
remains the registered follow-up on that axis. It is deferred, not
cancelled: a capacity step is worth taking **after** the collapse is
understood, because until then every capacity result is being read
through a preserve-on-peak artifact.

## 3. The mechanism under test

Rank 1 of the forensics campaign: **policy sharpening driving
argmax/decoding degradation.**

The measured substrate, from `checkpoint_autopsy.py` over every 10-iter
checkpoint of all 8 runs (receipt:
`runs/peak_instability/v29_calibration/calibration.txt`, Table 2):

| run | peak | \|logit\|max @ peak | @ 240 | × | batch ent @ peak | @ 240 | actor.W L2 × |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v27 s0 | 60 | 24.5 | 261.6 | 10.66 | 0.732 | 0.068 | 3.43 |
| v27 s1 | 50 | 14.1 | 219.0 | 15.52 | 0.782 | 0.152 | 3.39 |
| v27 s2 | 90 | 36.9 | 131.6 | 3.57 | 0.405 | 0.126 | 2.17 |
| v27 s3 | 60 | 21.8 | 169.6 | 7.77 | 0.756 | 0.087 | 3.57 |
| v28 s0 | 70 | 22.3 | 204.8 | 9.17 | 0.747 | 0.115 | 2.58 |
| v28 s1 | 60 | 19.0 | 152.1 | 7.99 | 0.607 | 0.109 | 3.45 |
| v28 s2 | 120 | 40.2 | 105.5 | 2.63 | 0.511 | 0.096 | 2.03 |
| v28 s3 | 90 | 30.6 | 117.7 | 3.84 | 0.419 | 0.149 | 2.26 |

Logit magnitude grows **2.63–15.52×** after the preserved peak and never
saturates; actor-head weight norm grows a further **2.03–3.57×**; policy
entropy on a fixed 800-state batch ends at **0.068–0.152 nats** against
a `ln 6 = 1.792` maximum. All 8/8. Only **29.1–39.4%** of argmax
decisions agree between the peak checkpoint and iter 240 on that fixed
batch (chance ≈ 16.7% for 6 actions), with the run+jump action's argmax
share falling 0.463 → 0.225 while bare "right" rises ~0.025 → ~0.12,
unanimous across 8/8.

**There is direct prior art on this exact level, and it is written into
the codebase.** `BackwardEntropyGuard`'s own docstring
(`src/training/trainer.py:320`) records the B4 v1 receipt from
2026-08-08:

> "On the 1-1 reverse curriculum the policy entropy fell 0.19 -> 0.04
> after ~iter 130, and the collapsed argmax then refused the final flag
> jump the sampled policy still took: cold-entrance greedy 0.02 against
> sampled 0.50/0.63, with the greedy episodes stalling at gx 3154-3157 —
> ten pixels short of the pole."

That is Rank 1's signature, measured on 1-1, banked eighteen days ago,
with the mechanism to fight it already built — **and never once applied
to a 1-1 config.**

### What is honestly NOT established

- Rank 1 is **unverified**. Only 4 of the forensics campaign's 18
  analyses carry an adversarial verification pass, and all 4 came back
  WEAKENED, none SURVIVES. The sharpening probe was not among the four.
- The **timing** of the turn is not explained by sharpening. Logit and
  weight-norm growth are smooth and linear straight through the peak
  with no inflection in any of the 8 runs — a background ratchet, not an
  event.
- The **two-phase structure** that makes Rank 1 interesting is **N=2 of
  8**. In v28 seed3, iters 90→120, honest greedy halves (0.667 → 0.333)
  while in-training `success_rate` holds at ceiling (1.000 → 0.950);
  only then (120→160) does `success_rate` crater 0.950 → 0.049. v28
  seed0 shows a weaker version. The other six runs have no dense honest
  evals.
- Sharpening is a candidate for **Phase 1 only**. Phase 2 has its own
  ranked candidate (Rank 2, critic degradation) and v29 does not test
  it. This is registered in the reading rule, not discovered afterwards.

**P1 exists precisely because of the first and third bullets.** It
converts the N=2 lead into an N=8 measurement for the price of half an
hour of eval.

## 4. Single variable: arm `backward_curriculum.entropy_guard` at floor 0.30

### The intervention

```yaml

  backward_curriculum:
    entropy_guard:
      floor: 0.30     # THE variable. No 1-1 config has ever set this.
      boost: 3.0      # house value, unchanged from the two configs that
                      # already ship this block (mario_1_2_backward.yaml,
                      # mario_1_3_backward.yaml -- both floor 0.08 boost 3.0)
```

Nothing else moves. `entropy_floor` stays `0.0`, `entropy_coef` stays
`0.005`, `entropy_coef_max` stays `0.05` (inert while `entropy_floor` is
0.0), `tile_hidden_dim` stays 96, the ladder stays
`checkpoints/backward_states/1-1-v27`, ReDo stays armed at
`tau=0.025`, all 250 iterations, all 4 seeds.

### Why this instrument and not the alternatives

Four candidates exist for braking the sharpening ratchet:

| Candidate | Change | Dose | Receipt in log? | Code touched |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 (chosen)** | `entropy_guard: {floor: 0.30, boost: 3.0}` | coef 0.005 → 0.015 (deterministic) | **yes** — configured line, ARM/DISARM lines, per-iter `coef` trace | none |
| 2 | `entropy_floor: 0.0 → 0.30` (adaptive controller) | coef 0.005 → 0.05 over ~6 iters | **no** — the controller at `trainer.py:9213` emits no log line and `entropy_coef` is not a `metrics.jsonl` field | none |
| 3 | KL anchor to a reference policy (`kl_anchor_checkpoint` + `kl_anchor_loss_coef`) | tunable | yes | none, but needs a donor checkpoint |
| 4 | Weight decay / logit penalty on the actor head | tunable | no | trainer + optimizer construction |

Candidate 2 is the tighter single-variable diff — one existing key, one
number — and the stronger dose. It is **rejected on observability**: it
would be impossible to prove from the receipts that the treatment ever
fired. That is this project's dead-knob void class (Phase 3's frozen
actor, the options 1e12 sentinel, 2-1 attempt 1 on the wrong ladder),
and paying a dose penalty to avoid it is the right trade. Candidate 2's
trigger is also `last_entropy` — a **single minibatch of ~2,400 per
iteration**, per the forensics' instrumentation audit — where the guard
keys on a 10-iteration trailing mean with hysteresis.

Candidate 3 anchors to a *reference policy*, which means importing a
donor's behaviour: a different experiment, with an adjudicated FAIL
history on this line (Variant A/B), and it cannot anchor to a peak the
run has not reached yet. Candidate 4 needs trainer code.

**Candidate 1 is registered as the primary and only arm.** Candidate 2
is named here as the graduated follow-up if the dose proves the binding
constraint — not run now, not budgeted now.

### Why floor 0.30 — calibrated, not guessed

Receipt: `runs/peak_instability/v29_calibration/calibration.txt`,
Tables 1 and 4, computed from the 8 controls' own `metrics.jsonl`.

Iteration at which the 10-iteration trailing mean of `ppo_entropy` first
falls below each candidate floor:

| run | peak | F0.20 | F0.25 | **F0.30** | F0.35 | F0.40 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v27 s0 | 60 | 119 | 112 | **107** | 97 | 90 |
| v27 s1 | 50 | 160 | 157 | **119** | 111 | 103 |
| v27 s2 | 90 | 194 | 174 | **147** | 122 | 109 |
| v27 s3 | 60 | 131 | 123 | **108** | 102 | 98 |
| v28 s0 | 70 | 145 | 137 | **123** | 114 | 109 |

A usable floor must satisfy two opposing constraints: arm **after** the

Against the behavioural floor — **registered definition: the first
iteration at or after the honest peak where the trailing-10 mean of
in-training `success_rate` falls below 0.10** (this reproduces the
forensics document's per-run floor iterations to within ±8 on all 8

| run | peak | arms @ 0.30 | behaviour floors | **lead** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v27 s0 | 60 | 107 | 128 | +21 |
| v27 s1 | 50 | 119 | 152 | +33 |
| v27 s2 | 90 | 147 | 187 | +40 |
| v27 s3 | 60 | 108 | 125 | +17 |

| v28 s1 | 60 | 116 | 155 | +39 |
| v28 s2 | 120 | 114 | 145 | +31 |
| v28 s3 | 90 | 124 | 151 | +27 |

**At floor 0.30 the guard arms 17–61 iterations before behaviour craters
in 8/8 control runs, and after the honest peak in 7 of 8.** Floor 0.25
arms as late as iter 174 (v27 s2), leaving too little runway. Floor 0.40
arms at iters 90–109 — on top of four of the eight peaks. 0.30 is the
only value in the grid that clears both constraints on the control data.

The one exception is registered rather than hidden: **v28 seed2 peaks at
iter 120 and would arm at 114 — six iterations early.** If that seed
under-performs its v28 pair, peak suppression is the first hypothesis,
and read M5 is the check.

Derived constants at `floor: 0.30`, all from `BackwardEntropyGuard`
defaults: `trailing: 10`, `min_samples: 5`, `recover_mult: 1.25` →
**disarm above 0.375**, `armed_history: 50`.

### Why the dose is deterministic here

With `entropy_floor: 0.0` the adaptive controller never runs and no
schedule rewrites `entropy_coef`, so the base is a constant 0.005 for
the whole run. The armed coefficient is therefore exactly
**0.005 × boost**, one number, printed every iteration in the
`[backward] ... coef` suffix. Note honestly what this means: at
`entropy_floor: 0.0` the `entropy_coef_max: 0.05` ceiling is **inert**,
so the boost is not bounded by anything except its own value. That is a
feature for interpretability and a liability if the dose is wrong —
which is what Preflight P2 measures before the budget is spent.

## 6. Preflights — the cheap falsifier runs FIRST

### P1 — the kill gate. Zero training compute. ~30 minutes.

**This is the falsifier. The 4-seed budget is not authorised until it
returns.**

Rank 1 predicts that in the Phase-1 window the policy is still capable
under its own sampling distribution and only the **argmax** has
degraded. Every checkpoint needed to test that is already on disk.

For all 8 archived runs, at 4 checkpoints per run spanning
peak → peak+60 on the 10-iteration grid (clamped to ≤240):

```
.venv/bin/python scripts/eval_game.py --game mario \
  --profile configs/mario_1_1_v2{7,8}_seed{N}.yaml \
  --checkpoint checkpoints/<run>/vanilla_ppo_iter_000{IT}.pt \
  --start-state runs/live_show/smb_4_4_micro/entrance_start.state \
  --episodes 50 --max-steps 1500 --sticky-prob 0.25 --start-jitter 16 \
  --eval-seed 0 --eval-workers 8 --eval-rng per-episode \
  --action-select {greedy,sample}
```

64 evaluations. Measured cost from the v28 gate receipts: **~25 s per
50-episode eval at `eval_workers 8`** — 16 gate evals spanned 6.6
minutes. 64 evals ≈ **27 minutes, zero training compute.**

`eval_workers` and `eval_rng` are pinned and recorded in every receipt.
This is not decoration: the forensics' verification pass found a **4×
swing in a near-zero clear-rate estimate driven purely by eval-harness
RNG configuration** (v27 seed2 read 0.167 at `eval_workers=2` and 0.04
once matched to its own historical protocol). Unpinned, P1 could return
its answer from harness configuration rather than from the policy.

**Pre-registered decision rule** (taken verbatim from the forensics §5,
where it was registered before this document existed):

- **PROMOTE — median(sampled/greedy) ≥ 1.5 in ≥6 of 8 runs.** Phase 1 is
  decoding degradation. Rank 1 is promoted to established, and **v29
  launches.**
- **KILL — sampled/greedy in [0.85, 1.15] in ≥6 of 8 runs.** Rank 1 is
  dead; Phase 1 is real capability loss and there is no sharpening
  effect for an entropy brake to act on. **v29 is withdrawn, not run.**
  The fork goes to Rank 2 (critic explained-variance instrumentation,
  then `value_coef` drop / critic freeze at iter ~100) and Rank 4
  (optimizer-moment reset at iter ~100, checkpoints already carry the
  state), in that order.
- **SPLIT — anything else.** Report the split; with N=8 a 4/4 outcome is
  a real result about seed heterogeneity, not a failed experiment. **v29
  does not launch on this registration**; a fresh one is written that
  accounts for the split.

Bundled into the same pass, because they cost nothing extra and both
were named as outstanding by the forensics:

- **P1b — control-curve extension.** The same greedy protocol at iters
  {180, 200, 220, 240} on all 8 runs, at eval seeds {0,1}, giving the
  late-window control that mechanism read M6 needs. ~27 min.
- **P1c — re-score the banked 0.767 control** through the canonical
  two-seed 100-episode gate. The forensics established this bar was set
  by a run that stopped at iter 159, at `num_envs: 24` (a CLI override
  contradicting its own config's 60), on a hand-picked checkpoint the
  automation never selected (automation chose iter 120; every document
  cites iter 140), at n=60 on a **single eval seed that has never been
  cross-checked**. Two evals, ~1 minute. Its outcome does **not** change
  v29's gate (see §7) — it is banked because that number currently
  adjudicates PASS/FAIL for this entire line of work.

### P2 — the dose probe. ~20 minutes of training compute. Conditional on P1 PROMOTE.

P1 can kill the hypothesis. P2 cannot — it can only kill the *dose*, and
it exists so that a null result at the gate cannot be silently confused
with an under-powered knob.

Question: on this architecture, does `boost: 3.0` (coef 0.005 → 0.015)
actually lift a collapsing trailing entropy back above the floor?

Procedure: copy `checkpoints/mario_1_1_v28_capacity_seed3/` through
`vanilla_ppo_iter_00150.pt` into a throwaway run directory, launch a
throwaway profile `configs/mario_1_1_v29_dose.yaml` (v29 seed-3 config
with `name: Mario 1-1 v29 dose probe`, which is what selects the
checkpoint directory) for 25 further iterations with auto-resume ON:

```
.venv/bin/python scripts/train_game.py --game mario \
  --profile configs/mario_1_1_v29_dose.yaml \
  --iters 175 --seed 3 --strict-config \
  2>&1 | tee runs/v29_stability/preflight/dose_boost3.log
```

At 24 s/iteration (measured: v28 seed3 ran 250 iterations in 100
minutes) this is **~10 minutes per arm.**

**Pre-registered dose rule:**

- The guard must ARM within 5–8 iterations of resume (it needs
  `min_samples: 5` observations to fill its window; v28 seed3's trailing
  entropy at iter 150 is well below 0.30).
- **DOSE OK** — the 10-iteration trailing mean of `ppo_entropy` rises by
  ≥ 0.05 nats within 25 armed iterations. `boost: 3.0` is registered for
  the real runs.
- **DOSE LOW** — it does not. Re-run the identical probe once at
  `boost: 10.0` (coef 0.005 → 0.050). If that clears the same bar,
  **`boost: 10.0` is registered for all four seeds and frozen there.**
  Both the test and the single fallback value are committed here, before
  either is run, so this is a pre-registered dose selection and not
  post-hoc tuning. No third value is authorised.
- **DOSE VOID** — neither arm lifts trailing entropy by 0.05 nats. The
  entropy lever cannot move this architecture and the registration is
  withdrawn; §12's V-DOSE applies.

**Registered limitation.** P2 resumes an already-collapsed trajectory,
which may respond differently from a fresh run arriving at iter ~120
with capability intact. P2 answers "can this boost move entropy on this
net at all" — a dose *floor*, not a dose guarantee. It is stated here so
it cannot be discovered conveniently later. The probe directory and its
checkpoints are throwaway and are deleted after the receipt is written.

## 7. Which control, and why

**v29's gates are scored against v28's 0.670 and against the measured
8-run collapse baseline. Not against the banked 0.767.**

| Control | Number | Protocol | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **v28 best-of-4** | **0.670** | 100 pooled eps, eval seeds {0,1}, `eval_workers 8`, `eval_rng per-episode`, 250 iters, `num_envs 60`, automation-selected checkpoint | **Gate 2** (peak suppression) |
| **8-run retention** | **0 of 8 seeds retain; max H_final 0.050; R = 0.000 in 4/4 qualifying seeds** | same | **Gate 1** (the headline) |
| Banked 0.767 | 0.767 | 60 eps, **eval seed 0 only**, run stopped at iter 159, `num_envs: 24` per the manifest against 60 in its config, hand-picked iter-140 checkpoint the automation never chose | reported for the record only |

The reason is not that 0.767 is fake. It is that it is **not
protocol-comparable**, and the forensics established this by reading
every row of `checkpoints/mario_1_1_backward/eval.jsonl`. Four process
differences all bias the same direction, and the decisive one is
structural: `configs/mario_1_1_backward.yaml` diffs against
`mario_1_1_v27_seed0.yaml` with **no difference** in architecture,
reward, `lr`, `entropy_coef`, `value_coef`, `rollout_steps`, or trainer
mode — it is the same recipe — and
`runs/mario_1_1_backward_seed0.log`, that identical config run to a full
250 iterations, sits at a train-time clear rate of **0.000–0.005 for the
entire back half.** The 0.767 run was stopped before its own decline
became visible.

Scoring a stability experiment against a bar produced by stopping early
would be scoring the treatment against the artifact it is designed to
remove. P1c re-scores 0.767 under the canonical gate and banks the
result; **v29's gates do not move with that outcome**, so the
re-scoring cannot be read as moving a goalpost in either direction.

## 8. Pre-registered gates

Two axes. Both are fixed here, before any compute. Scoring protocol,
identical for every number in this section:

```
.venv/bin/python scripts/eval_game.py --game mario \
  --profile configs/mario_1_1_v29_seedN.yaml --checkpoint <ckpt> \
  --start-state runs/live_show/smb_4_4_micro/entrance_start.state \
  --episodes 50 --max-steps 1500 --sticky-prob 0.25 --start-jitter 16 \
  --eval-seed {0,1} --action-select greedy \
  --eval-workers 8 --eval-rng per-episode
```

Per seed, two checkpoints: `winners/best.pt` (**H_peak**) and
`vanilla_ppo_iter_00240.pt` (**H_final**). 50 episodes × 2 eval seeds =
100 pooled per checkpoint. 16 evaluations, ~7 minutes.

### Gate 1 — RETENTION (the headline)

Per-seed definitions:

```
R      = H_final / H_peak
QUALIFY: H_peak  >= 0.30     (the seed found something worth keeping)
RETAIN : H_final >= 0.30  AND  R >= 0.50
```

- **PASS** — ≥ 3 of 4 seeds RETAIN.
- **MARGINAL** — 2 of 4 seeds RETAIN, **or** ≥ 3 seeds reach
  `H_final ≥ 0.20` with `R ≥ 0.35`.
- **FAIL** — neither of the above.

Measured control, all 8 archived runs, identical protocol
(`calibration.txt`, Table 3): **0 of 8 seeds RETAIN. Max H_final across
all eight runs is 0.050. Among the 4 seeds that QUALIFY, R is exactly
0.000 in 4 of 4.**

**Why R alone is not the gate.** v27 seed0 scores R = 0.500 on
0.020/0.040 — a ratio artifact of two near-zero numbers. A pure ratio
criterion rewards runs that never learned anything. The absolute
`H_final ≥ 0.30` floor is what makes RETAIN mean "still able to play the
level", and QUALIFY is defined on H_peak so a suppressed run cannot
launder itself into a retention pass.

**Why `R > 1` is allowed.** If the fix works, the honest curve may be
flat and the proxy-selected "peak" becomes an arbitrary draw from a
plateau, so H_final can exceed H_peak. That is a success, not an
anomaly, and it is reported as measured rather than clamped.

### Gate 2 — NO PEAK SUPPRESSION (guard-rail)

```
best-of-4 H_peak >= 0.500
```

v28's control best-of-4 is 0.670; at n=100 and p≈0.6 the binomial SE is
0.049, so 0.500 sits ~3.5 SE below the control. Below that line, the
treatment bought retention by capping capability, and Gate 1 alone must
not be reportable as a win.

This is the criterion that stops **"peaks at 0.05, ends at 0.04,
R = 0.80"** from scoring as a stability fix, and it is the registered
check on the one calibration risk named in §4 (v28 seed2's peak sits 6
iterations after its projected arm point).

### The headline verdict is the PAIR, and the asymmetry is registered now

> **A run that peaks at 0.67 and holds 0.35 to iter 240 (Gate 1 PASS,
> Gate 2 PASS) is the registered success of this experiment.**
>
> **A run that peaks at 0.75 and ends at 0.00 (Gate 1 FAIL, Gate 2 PASS)
> is a capability result and a stability FAIL. It is NOT a v29 success,
> it does not license continuing this line, and it may not be reported
> as "v29 raised the ceiling" without the retention number in the same
> sentence.**

A naive best-of-4 gate scores those two identically. That is exactly why
best-of-4 is demoted to a guard-rail here. Best-of-4 remains reported
for continuity with v27/v28 as a non-gate read (M7), and its known
defect is restated with it: it takes the max over seeds and is
structurally biased toward reporting improvement whenever *any* seed
improves.

## 9. Mechanism reads (registered, non-gate)

These turn a PASS or FAIL into a *why*. All are computed from receipts
the run produces anyway; none requires new machinery.

**M1 — Guard activity.** From the per-iteration
`[backward] iter N: ... | guard ARMED ent~X armed A/H coef C` suffix:
first arm iteration, total armed iterations, arm/disarm cycle count,
effective coefficient trace. *Registered expectation:* arms in the
window **107–147** (the control crossing range at floor 0.30) and stays
armed for the majority of the back half. An arm outside iters 90–180, or
more than 6 arm/disarm cycles (flapping), is reported as a calibration
finding in its own right.

**M2 — The sharpening ratchet.** `checkpoint_autopsy.py` on all 25
checkpoints per seed, against §3's control table:

| quantity | control (8/8) | v29 expectation under Rank-1 causality |
|---|---|---|
| `max_abs_logit` ratio 240/peak | 2.63–15.52× | ≤ 4× in ≥3/4 seeds |
| `mean_entropy` (fixed batch) @ 240 | 0.068–0.152 nats | ≥ 0.25 nats in ≥3/4 seeds |
| `l2__actor.weight` ratio 240/peak | 2.03–3.57× | < 2.0× in ≥3/4 seeds |
| argmax agreement peak↔240 | 29.1–39.4% | ≥ 60% in ≥3/4 seeds |

**M2 is the read that decides whether a Gate-1 FAIL is a refutation or
an under-dose**, and the reading rule in §10 turns on it.

**M3 — Greedy-vs-sampled gap at iter 240**, measured with P1's protocol
on the v29 finals and compared to P1's control values. Under Rank 1 the
treatment should *close* the gap, not merely raise both.

**M4 — Phase-2 timing.** Behavioural-floor iteration per §4's registered
definition, seed-paired against the control (v27: 128, 152, 187, 125;
v28: 184, 155, 145, 151). Under "sharpening drives both phases" this
moves later or never occurs. Under "sharpening drives Phase 1 only" it
is unchanged while M2 and M3 improve — which is the signature that hands
the fork to Rank 2.

**M5 — Ladder telemetry**, seed-paired against v28: iters-to-entrance,
rungs per 100 iterations, recovery-band clear rates. Entropy pressure
can slow a curriculum walk; this is the peak-suppression diagnostic and
the first thing to read if v28 seed2's pair under-performs.

**M6 — Late-window honest curve.** H at iters {180, 200, 220, 240}, 100
pooled each, against P1b's control extension. Distinguishes "held a
plateau" from "collapsed later".

**M7 — Best-of-4 for continuity** with v27 (0.530) and v28 (0.670),
reported with its bias restated.

**M8 — ReDo telemetry.** Expected identically zero (`0/96`, `0/32`, zero
recycles). Non-zero recycles under an entropy boost would be a genuinely
new fact about the interaction and is worth one line either way.

## 10. The falsifiable prediction

Registered before any v29 compute so it cannot be fitted afterwards:

> **Sharpening-is-the-driver** predicts that braking the ratchet
> preserves capability: M2 confirms the brake engaged (logit growth
> ≤ 4×, batch entropy ≥ 0.25 nats at iter 240, in ≥3/4 seeds) **and**
> Gate 1 PASSes **and** Gate 2 PASSes. Capability held, without capping
> the peak.
>
> **Sharpening-is-a-correlate** predicts the diagnostic opposite: M2
> confirms the brake engaged — logits held flat, entropy held up, argmax
> agreement high — **and capability collapses anyway.** That combination
> refutes sharpening as the driver of collapse, full stop. It is the
> most informative outcome this experiment can produce short of a PASS,
> because it eliminates the top-ranked candidate on positive evidence
> rather than on a null.

The third possibility is neither: **M2 shows the brake did not engage**
(logits still ratchet 8×, entropy still ends near 0.10 despite an armed
guard). That is an under-dose and it is **not a mechanism verdict** —
registered as such below so a weak knob cannot be written up as a
refutation.

### Reading rule — (Gate 1) × (Gate 2) × (M2 direction)

| Gate 1 retention | Gate 2 no-suppression | M2 brake engaged? | Registered verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PASS** | PASS | yes | **Sharpening CONFIRMED as the collapse driver.** The headline result of this line. Next: fold the guard into the standing 1-1 recipe, re-run the 0.80 capability bar with retention held, then port to 1-2/1-3 (which already ship the block at floor 0.08 and would need their own calibration). |
| **PASS** | PASS | no | Retention improved without the registered mechanism moving. Report as an **unexplained PASS** — a real result whose cause is not the one under test. Do not claim the mechanism; re-derive what else the boost changed before porting anything. |
| **PASS** | **FAIL** (<0.500) | yes | Stability bought by capping capability. **Stability mechanism CONFIRMED, capability REGRESSION.** Not a success. Named follow-up: re-register at floor 0.20 (arms at iters 119–194 per Table 1), which trades runway for peak protection. |
| **MARGINAL** | PASS | yes | Direction supports sharpening; 4 seeds cannot separate it from seed heterogeneity. Named follow-up: **4 more seeds at the identical config**, no config change, no re-tuning. |
| **FAIL** | PASS | **yes** | **Sharpening REFUTED as the driver of collapse.** The ratchet was stopped and capability died anyway. Fork to Rank 2 (critic): add explained variance to the metrics sink, then the `value_coef` drop / critic freeze at iter ~100, whose discriminating prediction is already written. |
| **FAIL** | PASS | **no** | **Under-dosed. Not a mechanism verdict.** Report as an inconclusive dose. Re-register once at the P2 fallback boost if P2 selected 3.0; if P2 already selected 10.0, the entropy lever is exhausted for 1-1 and the fork goes to Rank 2. |
| **FAIL** | FAIL | any | The treatment both suppressed the peak and failed to hold it — entropy pressure is actively harming this run. Abandon the entropy lever for 1-1. Fork to Rank 2, then Rank 4. |

M4 (Phase-2 timing) is the sub-read that splits the two FAIL-with-brake
rows in the writeup: if M2 and M3 improved and Gate 1 failed **but M6
shows the honest curve held a plateau materially longer before
cratering**, the honest description is "sharpening drives Phase 1 only,
Phase 2 has a different driver" rather than a flat refutation. That
distinction is registered now because it is exactly the conclusion that
would be tempting to reach for after the fact.

## 12. VOID conditions

A VOID is "the experiment never happened"; no verdict may be read.

- **V1 — single-variable diff check.** Machine diff of all four
  `configs/mario_1_1_v29_seed{N}.yaml` against
  `configs/mario_1_1_v28_seed{N}.yaml`: VOID if any key other than
  `name`, `description`, and the `backward_curriculum.entropy_guard`
  block differs. **The receipt at
  `runs/v29_stability/config_diff.json` must actually be written** —
  v28 registered this receipt path and never produced it, and a
  registered receipt that is never written is a gap even when the check
  itself was honest.
- **V2 — guard-configured receipt.** Every seed's startup log must print
  exactly:
  `[backward-guard] configured: arm when the 10-iter trailing policy entropy falls below 0.300 (needs 5 samples), entropy_coef x3.00 while armed, disarm above 0.375.`
  (`x10.00` if P2 selected the fallback boost). Any other floor, boost,
  trailing length, or disarm threshold VOIDs before the 4-seed spend.
  Do not "close enough" these numbers.
- **V3 — mechanism-armed-but-inert.** The guard must ARM at least once
  per seed, and while armed the `[backward] iter N ... coef C` suffix
  must read `0.01500` (or `0.05000` at the fallback boost) rather than
  the `0.00500` base. Check by grep —
  `grep -c '\[backward-guard\] ARMED' <log>` ≥ 1 and
  `grep -c 'guard ARMED' <log>` > 0 — or, equivalently, by adding a
  five-line entry to `scripts/experiment_preflight.py`'s `MECHANISMS`
  registry (`profile_path: ("reinforce", "backward_curriculum",
  "entropy_guard")`, `armed: r"\[backward-guard\] configured"`,
  `forbidden: None`); `profile_flag` already treats a non-empty dict as
  set, so no helper changes are needed. A seed whose guard never arms by
  iter 180 is **VOID-machinery for that seed**: in 8/8 control runs the
  trailing mean crossed 0.30 by iter 147, so a run where it does not is
  not the treatment, whatever else it is.
- **V4 — parameter-count receipt, inherited from v28.** Seed-0 startup
  must print `[policy] class=TilePolicyNetwork params=72039 (hidden=96,
  trunk/gru=32, features=712, actions=6)` verbatim. The width is *not*
  a v29 variable and any drift means the wrong parent was copied.
- **V5 — wrong-ladder guard.** Any seed whose `[backward] ENABLED` line
  does not name `checkpoints/backward_states/1-1-v27` with the full
  785-entry count is VOID for that seed — the class that voided 2-1
  attempt 1.
- **V6 — ladder liveness, per seed.** A seed whose tau has advanced 0
  rungs by iter 150 is VOID-machinery for that seed. If all four void
  this way the experiment is VOID, not FAIL.
- **V7 — eval lineage, extended.** Any gate eval not run with
  `--start-state runs/live_show/smb_4_4_micro/entrance_start.state` and
  the registered flags is discarded, not reported. **New in v29:**
  `eval_workers: 8` and `eval_rng: per-episode` must appear in every
  receipt JSON. The forensics measured a **4× swing in a near-zero clear
  rate from eval-harness RNG configuration alone**; an unpinned receipt
  is not comparable to a pinned one and may not be pooled with it.
- **V8 — preserve-on-peak must be live.** Gate 1 reads
  `winners/best.pt`. A seed without one cannot be scored on the
  retention gate at all, and preserve-on-peak stays mandatory in every
  arm of this line.
- **V9 — preflight order.** Launching the 4-seed budget before P1
  returns, or after P1 returns KILL or SPLIT, VOIDs the experiment
  regardless of what it produces. This is the binding project rule and
  it is a VOID, not a note.
- **V10 — ReDo unchanged.** `redo_enabled: true`, `redo_tau: 0.025`,
  `redo_check_every_iters: 1` must be identical to v28 and
  `[redo] ENABLED tau=0.025` must appear in every log with
  `[redo] disabled` in none. Turning ReDo off would be a second variable
  and would reopen the dormancy confound.
- **V-DOSE (soft).** If P2 returns DOSE VOID — neither `boost: 3.0` nor
  `boost: 10.0` lifts the trailing entropy by ≥ 0.05 nats within 25
  armed iterations — the entropy lever cannot move this architecture.
  The registration is withdrawn before the main spend and the fork goes
  to Rank 2. This is a withdrawal, not a FAIL: no mechanism verdict may
  be read from it.

## 14. What this document does not do

It does not consult DR. The mechanism is already built, already tested,
and already shipped on two sibling configs; the only judgment calls
(which instrument, which floor) are settled in §4 against measured
control data with the receipt banked, not by external adjudication.

It does not re-open the capacity question. `tile_hidden_dim` stays 96
and v28's balanced-widen follow-up is deferred, not cancelled.

It does not touch the ladder, the reward, `merge_recovery_ladder.py`,
`recovery_assay.py`, or `mine_recovery_tapes.py`. The 785-rung ladder is
reused byte-for-byte from v27/v28.

It does not add the five instrumentation scalars the forensics
recommended (`clip_fraction`, `approx_kl`, `grad_norm`,
`adv_mean`/`adv_std`, critic explained variance). Each is a one-line
addition and each would be a change to code that runs during training —
they belong to the Rank 2 fork, which needs explained variance to be
answerable at all, and adding them here would weaken the zero-code claim
for no gain to this experiment. **They should land before any Rank 2
run**, and that is the recommendation this document makes on the way
past, not a deliverable it claims.

It does not decide anything about 1-2, 1-3, or 1-4. Those levels ship
this same guard at `floor: 0.08` — a value calibrated for a different
entropy trajectory — and would each need their own Table-1 calibration
against their own runs before any floor is transplanted.

And it does not promise that a PASS closes the question. Rank 1 is one
of three candidates the forensics campaign left live, it is unverified,
and its supporting two-phase finding is N=2 of 8. A PASS here would be
the first mechanism *established* in this line. A FAIL with the brake
demonstrably engaged would be almost as valuable, and the fork it hands
to is already written.
