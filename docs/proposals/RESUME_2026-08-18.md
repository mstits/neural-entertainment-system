## Known limitation to fix first in the morning

`hazard_phase1`, `replay_sweep_full` and `suite_check` have no
filesystem-derived completion check, so nothing marks them done. They
will re-run until `MAX_ATTEMPTS_PER_ACTION` (2) and then be abandoned.
Bounded and safe — no runaway — but wrong: `suite_check` in particular
should be repeatable rather than capped. The fix is a `completed` write
when a launched action's process exits cleanly, plus an exemption for
recurring tasks.

Everything driven by `honest_eval_done`, `level_has_campaign` and the
W-chain resolves correctly, because those read the filesystem.
